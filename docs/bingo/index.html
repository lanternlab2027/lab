How to make a 2027 goal bingo card

  1. Pick a size. 5×5 is the classic (24 goals plus a free space). 4×4 and 3×3 are easier to finish.
  2. Fill the squares. Mix a few big goals with lots of small, fun ones. Tap the idea categories for inspiration.
  3. Choose a theme and download a printable PDF for the fridge, or a phone wallpaper so you see it every day.
  4. Bookmark your link. Each time you finish a goal, open it, switch to Check off and tap the square. Five in a row is a bingo.

Questions

What is goal bingo?

Goal bingo (or resolution bingo) swaps one big new year resolution for a grid of smaller goals. Instead of "get healthy", you get 24 concrete things like "run a 5K", "cook a new vegetable" and "call grandma monthly". Finishing a row, column or diagonal is a bingo, and filling the whole card is a blackout.

Where is my card saved?

In the link itself (the part after the #), and in this browser's storage. The part after # is never sent to any server, including ours, and there's no account or database. That's why you should bookmark the link or email it to yourself: if you clear your browser and lose the link, the card is gone.

How does group bingo work?

The group link only carries a group name, theme and card size. Each friend makes their own card and gets their own private link. If you want to see each other's progress, share your card images (they show "7 of 24 done").

Can I print it?

Yes. "Print PDF" makes a US Letter page that fills the sheet. For the least ink, choose the Mono theme.
