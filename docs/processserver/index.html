What the real exam is like

Questions and pass mark
The exam has 35 multiple-choice questions, and you need at least 25 correct to pass. It tests the City and State laws and rules you must know to serve process legally in New York City.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 4–5
Time limit
DCWP does not publish a time limit in its checklist or exam FAQ. Ask ExamBuilder when you book. The mock exam on this site uses a practice pace of one minute per question.
Who takes it
Both new and renewing applicants must pass the exam. You need the license if you serve process five or more times in a calendar year. Attorneys admitted in New York, and government employees acting within the scope of their jobs, are exempt.
Sources: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 1; DCWP license checklist
Fees
The exam fee is $75. It is nonrefundable and separate from the license fee, and a second attempt costs nothing extra. The license runs for 2 years and expires on February 28 of even years, so the license fee is $85 to $425 depending on when you apply ($340 for a full 24 months). Card payments carry a nonrefundable 2% convenience fee.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; Educational Materials, 6 RCNY § 2-232d, PDF p. 10
Attempts
You get two attempts in total. After a first failure, you must wait 24 hours before booking again. If you fail twice, you must reapply for the license and pay the license and exam fees again. If you miss an exam without rescheduling by 5:00 p.m. the day before, it counts as a fail.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 2–3
Booking and where
ExamBuilder runs the exam. You can book 24 hours after DCWP processes your completed application, online at dcaschedule.exambuilder.com or by phone at (646) 974-8244. You then have 60 days to take and pass it. Test centers in multiple locations citywide are open 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., seven days a week. Applicants granted a disability or language accommodation test at the DCWP Licensing Center, 42 Broadway.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 1–2
On the day
Bring one government-issued photo ID with your signature. A driver's license, passport, IDNYC or your DCWP Process Server Individual license all count. You get your score and a pass or fail result as soon as you finish.
Source: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 3
