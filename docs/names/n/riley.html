Riley

Steady

Riley was the 48th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 4,441 girls. It peaked in 2016, when about 1 in 248 girls got the name (7,159 babies). Its best-ever rank was #22 in 2016. Since 1880, 43% of babies named Riley have been boys.
