Akira

Steady

Akira was the 983rd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 257 girls. It peaked in 2008, when about 1 in 4,460 girls got the name (424 babies). Its best-ever rank was #707 in 2008. Since 1880, 21% of babies named Akira have been boys.
