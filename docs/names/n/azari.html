Azari

Rising

Azari was the 766th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 354 girls. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 3,866 girls got the name (415 babies). Its best-ever rank was #696 in 2023. Since 1880, 29% of babies named Azari have been boys.
