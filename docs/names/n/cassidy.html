Cassidy

Falling

Cassidy was the 541st most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 558 girls. It peaked in 1999, when about 1 in 522 girls got the name (3,395 babies). Its best-ever rank was #99 in 1999. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Cassidy have been boys.
