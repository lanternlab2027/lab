Dakota

Steady

Dakota was the 319th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,074 boys. It peaked in 1995, when about 1 in 290 boys got the name (6,575 babies). Its best-ever rank was #56 in 1995. Since 1880, 31% of babies named Dakota have been girls.
