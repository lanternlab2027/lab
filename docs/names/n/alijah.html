Alijah

Rising

Alijah was the 469th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 651 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 2,296 boys got the name (762 babies). Its best-ever rank was #408 in 2021. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Alijah have been girls.
