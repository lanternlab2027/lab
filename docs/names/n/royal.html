Royal

Rising

Royal was the 476th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 640 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 1,970 boys got the name (888 babies). Its best-ever rank was #279 in 1889. Since 1880, 18% of babies named Royal have been girls.
