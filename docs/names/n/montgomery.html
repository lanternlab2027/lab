Montgomery

Rising

Montgomery was the 962nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 241 boys. That is its highest share of births on record. Its best-ever rank was #778 in 1883. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Montgomery have been girls.
