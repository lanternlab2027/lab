Karter

Steady

Karter was the 270th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,265 boys. It peaked in 2020, when about 1 in 738 boys got the name (2,340 babies). Its best-ever rank was #162 in 2020. Since 1880, 13% of babies named Karter have been girls.
