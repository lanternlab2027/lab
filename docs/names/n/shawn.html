Shawn

Falling

Shawn was the 682nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 399 boys. It peaked in 1973, when about 1 in 136 boys got the name (11,451 babies). Its best-ever rank was #27 in 1973. Since 1880, 10% of babies named Shawn have been girls.
