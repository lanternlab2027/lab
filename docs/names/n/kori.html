Kori

Steady

Kori was the 978th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 260 girls. It peaked in 2016, when about 1 in 3,731 girls got the name (475 babies). Its best-ever rank was #631 in 2016. Since 1880, 10% of babies named Kori have been boys.
