Salem

Rising

Salem was the 436th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 711 girls. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 2,186 girls got the name (734 babies). Its best-ever rank was #417 in 2023. Since 1880, 42% of babies named Salem have been boys.
