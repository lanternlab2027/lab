Kalani

Rising

Kalani was the 387th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 800 girls. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 1,711 girls got the name (959 babies). Its best-ever rank was #339 in 2021. Since 1880, 18% of babies named Kalani have been boys.
