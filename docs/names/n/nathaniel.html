Nathaniel

Classic

Nathaniel was the 140th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,565 boys. It peaked in 2002, when about 1 in 285 boys got the name (6,813 babies). Its best-ever rank was #60 in 1998.
