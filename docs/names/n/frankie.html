Frankie

Vintage revival

Frankie was the 552nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 549 girls. It peaked in 1934, when about 1 in 1,770 girls got the name (590 babies). Its best-ever rank was #231 in 1888. Since 1880, 53% of babies named Frankie have been boys.
