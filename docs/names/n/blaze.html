Blaze

Steady

Blaze was the 781st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 326 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 5,091 boys got the name (336 babies). Its best-ever rank was #707 in 2015. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Blaze have been girls.
