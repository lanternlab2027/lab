Brooklyn

Falling

Brooklyn was the 118th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,300 girls. It peaked in 2011, when about 1 in 245 girls got the name (7,178 babies). Its best-ever rank was #21 in 2011.
