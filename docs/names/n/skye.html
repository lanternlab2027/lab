Skye

Steady

Skye was the 532nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 569 girls. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 2,014 girls got the name (887 babies). Its best-ever rank was #368 in 2014. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Skye have been boys.
