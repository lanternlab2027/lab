Darcy

Rising

Darcy was the 857th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 316 girls. It peaked in 1968, when about 1 in 2,547 girls got the name (644 babies). Its best-ever rank was #349 in 1968. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Darcy have been boys.
