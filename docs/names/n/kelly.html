Kelly

Falling

Kelly was the 852nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 319 girls. It peaked in 1977, when about 1 in 84 girls got the name (18,234 babies). Its best-ever rank was #10 in 1977. Since 1880, 15% of babies named Kelly have been boys.
