Kendall

Falling

Kendall was the 328th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 932 girls. It peaked in 2012, when about 1 in 671 girls got the name (2,625 babies). Its best-ever rank was #116 in 2012. Since 1880, 35% of babies named Kendall have been boys.
