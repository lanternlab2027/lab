Ariel

Falling

Ariel was the 356th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 851 girls. It peaked in 1991, when about 1 in 347 girls got the name (5,411 babies). Its best-ever rank was #66 in 1991. Since 1880, 22% of babies named Ariel have been boys.
