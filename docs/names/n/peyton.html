Peyton

Falling

Peyton was the 199th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,559 girls. It peaked in 2009, when about 1 in 345 girls got the name (5,318 babies). Its best-ever rank was #42 in 2009. Since 1880, 37% of babies named Peyton have been boys.
