Zaire

Rising

Zaire was the 503rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 601 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 2,612 boys got the name (655 babies). Its best-ever rank was #466 in 2023. Since 1880, 13% of babies named Zaire have been girls.
