Robin

Rising

Robin was the 704th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 400 girls. It peaked in 1961, when about 1 in 129 girls got the name (15,653 babies). Its best-ever rank was #25 in 1962. Since 1880, 14% of babies named Robin have been boys.
