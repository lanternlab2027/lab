Lilly

Vintage revival

Lilly was the 252nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,218 girls. It peaked in 2011, when about 1 in 612 girls got the name (2,873 babies). Its best-ever rank was #103 in 2011.
