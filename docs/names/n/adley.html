Adley

Rising

Adley was the 805th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 339 girls. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 2,686 girls got the name (611 babies). Its best-ever rank was #509 in 2021. Since 1880, 10% of babies named Adley have been boys.
