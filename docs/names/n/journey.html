Journey

Steady

Journey was the 411th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 757 girls. It peaked in 2018, when about 1 in 1,218 girls got the name (1,397 babies). Its best-ever rank was #226 in 2018. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Journey have been boys.
