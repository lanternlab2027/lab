Ira

Rising

Ira was the 822nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 301 boys. It peaked in 1881, when about 1 in 352 boys got the name (286 babies). Its best-ever rank was #57 in 1881. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Ira have been girls.
