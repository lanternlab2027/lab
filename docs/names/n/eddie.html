Eddie

Falling

Eddie was the 861st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 280 boys. It peaked in 1910, when about 1 in 326 boys got the name (595 babies). Its best-ever rank was #59 in 1910. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Eddie have been girls.
