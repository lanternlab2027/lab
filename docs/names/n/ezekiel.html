Ezekiel

Rising

Ezekiel was the 61st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 5,091 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 288 boys got the name (6,053 babies). Its best-ever rank was #48 in 2022.
