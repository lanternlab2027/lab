Allie

Vintage revival

Allie was the 569th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 529 girls. It peaked in 1889, when about 1 in 756 girls got the name (236 babies). Its best-ever rank was #134 in 1883. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Allie have been boys.
