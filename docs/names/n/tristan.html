Tristan

Falling

Tristan was the 309th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,103 boys. It peaked in 1996, when about 1 in 347 boys got the name (5,458 babies). Its best-ever rank was #68 in 1996. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Tristan have been girls.
