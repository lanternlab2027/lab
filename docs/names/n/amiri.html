Amiri

Rising

Amiri was the 604th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 472 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 2,870 boys got the name (596 babies). Its best-ever rank was #506 in 2023. Since 1880, 33% of babies named Amiri have been girls.
