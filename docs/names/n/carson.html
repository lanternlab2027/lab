Carson

Falling

Carson was the 124th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,883 boys. It peaked in 2018, when about 1 in 345 boys got the name (5,259 babies). Its best-ever rank was #70 in 2018. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Carson have been girls.
