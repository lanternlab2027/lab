Mackenzie

Falling

Mackenzie was the 228th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,343 girls. It peaked in 2001, when about 1 in 260 girls got the name (6,934 babies). Its best-ever rank was #40 in 2001. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Mackenzie have been boys.
