Braylen

Falling

Braylen was the 725th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 366 boys. It peaked in 2011, when about 1 in 1,879 boys got the name (1,011 babies). Its best-ever rank was #316 in 2011. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Braylen have been girls.
