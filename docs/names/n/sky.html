Sky

Steady

Sky was the 949th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 272 girls. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 4,052 girls got the name (405 babies). Its best-ever rank was #712 in 2021. Since 1880, 30% of babies named Sky have been boys.
