Harlem

Rising

Harlem was the 971st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 236 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 6,735 boys got the name (254 babies). Its best-ever rank was #906 in 2020. Since 1880, 34% of babies named Harlem have been girls.
