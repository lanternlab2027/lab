Teddy

Vintage revival

Teddy was the 823rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 301 boys. It peaked in 1933, when about 1 in 2,083 boys got the name (476 babies). Its best-ever rank was #239 in 1933. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Teddy have been girls.
