Penelope

Vintage revival

Penelope was the 22nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 6,225 girls. It peaked in 2019, when about 1 in 249 girls got the name (6,734 babies). Its best-ever rank was #21 in 2022.
