Raylan

Steady

Raylan was the 802nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 311 boys. It peaked in 2015, when about 1 in 3,042 boys got the name (630 babies). Its best-ever rank was #473 in 2015. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Raylan have been girls.
