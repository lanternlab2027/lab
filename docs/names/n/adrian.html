Adrian

Falling

Adrian was the 74th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 4,464 boys. It peaked in 2008, when about 1 in 257 boys got the name (7,938 babies). Its best-ever rank was #56 in 2008. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Adrian have been girls.
