Jamison

Falling

Jamison was the 786th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 324 boys. It peaked in 2019, when about 1 in 2,549 boys got the name (704 babies). Its best-ever rank was #408 in 1973. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Jamison have been girls.
