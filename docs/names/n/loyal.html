Loyal

Rising

Loyal was the 983rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 233 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 4,631 boys got the name (377 babies). Its best-ever rank was #555 in 1890. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Loyal have been girls.
