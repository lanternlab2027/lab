Finnley

Rising

Finnley was the 757th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 340 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 3,715 boys got the name (470 babies). Its best-ever rank was #591 in 2021. Since 1880, 31% of babies named Finnley have been girls.
