Elsie

Vintage revival

Elsie was the 123rd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,236 girls. It peaked in 1896, when about 1 in 127 girls got the name (1,878 babies). Its best-ever rank was #31 in 1896.
