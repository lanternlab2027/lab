Hunter

Falling

Hunter was the 130th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,761 boys. It peaked in 2000, when about 1 in 157 boys got the name (12,538 babies). Its best-ever rank was #35 in 2000. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Hunter have been girls.
