Fallon

Rising

Fallon was the 779th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 349 girls. It peaked in 1982, when about 1 in 2,771 girls got the name (611 babies). Its best-ever rank was #376 in 1982. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Fallon have been boys.
