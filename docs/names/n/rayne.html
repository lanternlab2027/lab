Rayne

Steady

Rayne was the 906th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 293 girls. It peaked in 2019, when about 1 in 4,359 girls got the name (385 babies). Its best-ever rank was #736 in 2019. Since 1880, 14% of babies named Rayne have been boys.
