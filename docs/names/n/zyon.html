Zyon

Rising

Zyon was the 947th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 249 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 6,175 boys got the name (277 babies). Its best-ever rank was #862 in 2023. Since 1880, 14% of babies named Zyon have been girls.
