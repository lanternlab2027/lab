Noel

Classic

Noel was the 478th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 635 boys. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 2,132 boys got the name (901 babies). Its best-ever rank was #267 in 1938. Since 1880, 23% of babies named Noel have been girls.
