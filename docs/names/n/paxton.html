Paxton

Steady

Paxton was the 338th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 988 boys. It peaked in 2016, when about 1 in 952 boys got the name (1,993 babies). Its best-ever rank was #203 in 2016. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Paxton have been girls.
