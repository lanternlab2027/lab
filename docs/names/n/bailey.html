Bailey

Falling

Bailey was the 209th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,446 girls. It peaked in 1998, when about 1 in 340 girls got the name (5,198 babies). Its best-ever rank was #60 in 1998. Since 1880, 16% of babies named Bailey have been boys.
