Ryland

Falling

Ryland was the 706th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 382 boys. It peaked in 2013, when about 1 in 2,602 boys got the name (727 babies). Its best-ever rank was #407 in 2013. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Ryland have been girls.
