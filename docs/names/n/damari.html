Damari

Rising

Damari was the 958th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 243 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 6,204 boys got the name (282 babies). Its best-ever rank was #782 in 2016. Since 1880, 13% of babies named Damari have been girls.
