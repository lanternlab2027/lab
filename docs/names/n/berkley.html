Berkley

Rising

Berkley was the 888th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 301 girls. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 4,635 girls got the name (354 babies). Its best-ever rank was #798 in 2021. Since 1880, 29% of babies named Berkley have been boys.
