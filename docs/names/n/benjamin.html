Benjamin

Vintage revival

Benjamin was the 11th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 9,762 boys. It peaked in 1981, when about 1 in 125 boys got the name (14,360 babies). Its best-ever rank was #6 in 2016.
