Keaton

Falling

Keaton was the 771st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 333 boys. It peaked in 2011, when about 1 in 2,333 boys got the name (814 babies). Its best-ever rank was #353 in 2000. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Keaton have been girls.
