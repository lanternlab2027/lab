Lee

Falling

Lee was the 727th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 366 boys. It peaked in 1900, when about 1 in 223 boys got the name (675 babies). Its best-ever rank was #39 in 1900. Since 1880, 21% of babies named Lee have been girls.
