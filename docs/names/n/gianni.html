Gianni

Rising

Gianni was the 524th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 571 boys. It peaked in 2020, when about 1 in 1,924 boys got the name (897 babies). Its best-ever rank was #354 in 2020. Since 1880, 18% of babies named Gianni have been girls.
