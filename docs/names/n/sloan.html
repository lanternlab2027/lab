Sloan

Rising

Sloan was the 840th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 324 girls. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 3,096 girls got the name (530 babies). Its best-ever rank was #572 in 2021. Since 1880, 26% of babies named Sloan have been boys.
