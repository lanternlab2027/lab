Carmen

Steady

Carmen was the 389th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 798 girls. It peaked in 1968, when about 1 in 723 girls got the name (2,270 babies). Its best-ever rank was #141 in 1968. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Carmen have been boys.
