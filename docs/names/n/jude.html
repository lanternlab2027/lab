Jude

Steady

Jude was the 155th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,384 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 698 boys got the name (2,506 babies). Its best-ever rank was #152 in 2019. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Jude have been girls.
