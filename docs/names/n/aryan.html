Aryan

Steady

Aryan was the 872nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 275 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 4,819 boys got the name (355 babies). Its best-ever rank was #613 in 2007. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Aryan have been girls.
