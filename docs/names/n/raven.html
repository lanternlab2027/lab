Raven

Rising

Raven was the 404th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 771 girls. It peaked in 1993, when about 1 in 792 girls got the name (2,285 babies). Its best-ever rank was #139 in 1993. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Raven have been boys.
