Parker

Falling

Parker was the 102nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 3,477 boys. It peaked in 2013, when about 1 in 333 boys got the name (5,672 babies). Its best-ever rank was #72 in 2015. Since 1880, 20% of babies named Parker have been girls.
