Jesiah

Rising

Jesiah was the 865th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 277 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 5,487 boys got the name (314 babies). Its best-ever rank was #804 in 2024. Since 1880, 15% of babies named Jesiah have been girls.
