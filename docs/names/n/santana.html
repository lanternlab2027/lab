Santana

Rising

Santana was the 855th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 285 boys. It peaked in 2020, when about 1 in 3,307 boys got the name (522 babies). Its best-ever rank was #544 in 2020. Since 1880, 43% of babies named Santana have been girls.
