Charlie

Vintage revival

Charlie was the 145th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,460 boys. It peaked in 1900, when about 1 in 134 boys got the name (1,124 babies). Its best-ever rank was #20 in 1881. Since 1880, 18% of babies named Charlie have been girls.
