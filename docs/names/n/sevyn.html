Sevyn

Rising

Sevyn was the 575th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 518 girls. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 2,891 girls got the name (555 babies). Its best-ever rank was #547 in 2023. Since 1880, 37% of babies named Sevyn have been boys.
