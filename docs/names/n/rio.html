Rio

Rising

Rio was the 532nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 563 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 2,905 boys got the name (593 babies). Its best-ever rank was #517 in 2024. Since 1880, 29% of babies named Rio have been girls.
