Sawyer

Steady

Sawyer was the 122nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,941 boys. It peaked in 2017, when about 1 in 444 boys got the name (4,171 babies). Its best-ever rank was #94 in 2015. Since 1880, 22% of babies named Sawyer have been girls.
