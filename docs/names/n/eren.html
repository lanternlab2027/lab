Eren

Rising

Eren was the 882nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 270 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 6,242 boys got the name (276 babies). Its best-ever rank was #874 in 2024. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Eren have been girls.
