Kennedy

Steady

Kennedy was the 85th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,933 girls. It peaked in 2015, when about 1 in 395 girls got the name (4,523 babies). Its best-ever rank was #54 in 2014. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Kennedy have been boys.
