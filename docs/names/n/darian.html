Darian

Steady

Darian was the 928th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 254 boys. It peaked in 1994, when about 1 in 2,867 boys got the name (674 babies). Its best-ever rank was #358 in 1994. Since 1880, 27% of babies named Darian have been girls.
