Kylan

Rising

Kylan was the 579th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 507 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 3,031 boys got the name (576 babies). Its best-ever rank was #515 in 2022. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Kylan have been girls.
