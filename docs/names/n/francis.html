Francis

Rising

Francis was the 420th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 769 boys. It peaked in 1915, when about 1 in 152 boys got the name (5,594 babies). Its best-ever rank was #29 in 1915. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Francis have been girls.
