Milan

Rising

Milan was the 242nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,418 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 1,109 boys got the name (1,554 babies). Its best-ever rank was #230 in 2023. Since 1880, 34% of babies named Milan have been girls.
