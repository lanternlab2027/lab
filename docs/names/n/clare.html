Clare

Falling

Clare was the 924th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 283 girls. It peaked in 1884, when about 1 in 3,395 girls got the name (38 babies). Its best-ever rank was #320 in 1884. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Clare have been boys.
