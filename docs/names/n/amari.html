Amari

Rising

Amari was the 185th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,000 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 706 boys got the name (2,422 babies). Its best-ever rank was #151 in 2023. Since 1880, 40% of babies named Amari have been girls.
