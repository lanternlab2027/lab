Vivian

Vintage revival

Vivian was the 72nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 3,362 girls. It peaked in 1920, when about 1 in 290 girls got the name (4,128 babies). Its best-ever rank was #64 in 1920.
