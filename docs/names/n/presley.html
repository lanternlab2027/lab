Presley

Steady

Presley was the 225th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,362 girls. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 1,025 girls got the name (1,743 babies). Its best-ever rank was #188 in 2015. Since 1880, 13% of babies named Presley have been boys.
