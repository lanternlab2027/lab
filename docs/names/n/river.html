River

Rising

River was the 111th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 3,139 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 499 boys got the name (3,500 babies). Its best-ever rank was #105 in 2022. Since 1880, 33% of babies named River have been girls.
