Taylor

Falling

Taylor was the 403rd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 772 girls. It peaked in 1993, when about 1 in 85 girls got the name (21,270 babies). Its best-ever rank was #6 in 1994. Since 1880, 26% of babies named Taylor have been boys.
