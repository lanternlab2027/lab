Malcolm

Classic

Malcolm was the 272nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,257 boys. It peaked in 1992, when about 1 in 1,336 boys got the name (1,495 babies). Its best-ever rank was #205 in 1928.
