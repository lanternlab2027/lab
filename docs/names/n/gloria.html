Gloria

Falling

Gloria was the 655th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 437 girls. It peaked in 1945, when about 1 in 118 girls got the name (11,037 babies). Its best-ever rank was #20 in 1925.

Trend: Falling. Long-term faller: its share of births is down 96% from its peak decade (the 1940s), though it has held about level over the last ten years.
