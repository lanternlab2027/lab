Ivey

Rising

Ivey was the 926th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 282 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 4,526 girls got the name (358 babies). Its best-ever rank was #789 in 2024. Since 1880, 27% of babies named Ivey have been boys.
