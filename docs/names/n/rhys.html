Rhys

Rising

Rhys was the 412th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 778 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 1,810 boys got the name (952 babies). Its best-ever rank was #353 in 2023. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Rhys have been girls.
