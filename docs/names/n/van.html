Van

Steady

Van was the 972nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 236 boys. It peaked in 1891, when about 1 in 2,108 boys got the name (48 babies). Its best-ever rank was #234 in 1891. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Van have been girls.
