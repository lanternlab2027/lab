Quincy

Steady

Quincy was the 654th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 422 boys. It peaked in 1977, when about 1 in 2,295 boys got the name (716 babies). Its best-ever rank was #273 in 1977. Since 1880, 16% of babies named Quincy have been girls.
