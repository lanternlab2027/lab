Elliot

Rising

Elliot was the 151st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,417 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 690 boys got the name (2,532 babies). Its best-ever rank was #150 in 2022. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Elliot have been girls.
