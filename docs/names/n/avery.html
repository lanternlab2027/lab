Avery

Falling

Avery was the 37th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 5,267 girls. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 187 girls got the name (9,578 babies). Its best-ever rank was #12 in 2013. Since 1880, 28% of babies named Avery have been boys.
