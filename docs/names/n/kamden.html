Kamden

Falling

Kamden was the 718th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 375 boys. It peaked in 2013, when about 1 in 2,102 boys got the name (900 babies). Its best-ever rank was #352 in 2013. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Kamden have been girls.
