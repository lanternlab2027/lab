Harley

Steady

Harley was the 418th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 748 girls. It peaked in 2017, when about 1 in 1,127 girls got the name (1,532 babies). Its best-ever rank was #202 in 2017. Since 1880, 57% of babies named Harley have been boys.
