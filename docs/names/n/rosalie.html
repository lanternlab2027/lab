Rosalie

Vintage revival

Rosalie was the 171st most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,797 girls. It peaked in 1938, when about 1 in 294 girls got the name (3,758 babies). Its best-ever rank was #66 in 1938.
