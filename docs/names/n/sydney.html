Sydney

Falling

Sydney was the 348th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 873 girls. It peaked in 2000, when about 1 in 177 girls got the name (10,243 babies). Its best-ever rank was #23 in 1999. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Sydney have been boys.
