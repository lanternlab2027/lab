Royce

Rising

Royce was the 500th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 606 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 2,160 boys got the name (810 babies). Its best-ever rank was #283 in 1935. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Royce have been girls.
