Uriah

Steady

Uriah was the 650th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 427 boys. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 3,436 boys got the name (559 babies). Its best-ever rank was #505 in 2014. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Uriah have been girls.
