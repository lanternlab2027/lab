Elisha

Vintage revival

Elisha was the 789th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 322 boys. It peaked in 1883, when about 1 in 2,491 boys got the name (42 babies). Its best-ever rank was #255 in 1883. Since 1880, 48% of babies named Elisha have been girls.
