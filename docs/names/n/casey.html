Casey

Rising

Casey was the 320th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,072 boys. It peaked in 1988, when about 1 in 474 boys got the name (4,039 babies). Its best-ever rank was #80 in 1987. Since 1880, 40% of babies named Casey have been girls.
