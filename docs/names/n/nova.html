Nova

Rising

Nova was the 46th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 4,657 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 277 girls got the name (5,927 babies). Its best-ever rank was #32 in 2021. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Nova have been boys.
