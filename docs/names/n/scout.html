Scout

Rising

Scout was the 997th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 254 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 3,859 girls got the name (425 babies). Its best-ever rank was #691 in 2022. Since 1880, 29% of babies named Scout have been boys.
