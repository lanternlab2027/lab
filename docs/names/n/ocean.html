Ocean

Rising

Ocean was the 720th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 375 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 3,552 boys got the name (485 babies). Its best-ever rank was #592 in 2024. Since 1880, 46% of babies named Ocean have been girls.
