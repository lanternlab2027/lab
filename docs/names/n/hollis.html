Hollis

Rising

Hollis was the 894th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 266 boys. It peaked in 1908, when about 1 in 2,756 boys got the name (56 babies). Its best-ever rank was #292 in 1908. Since 1880, 26% of babies named Hollis have been girls.
