Skylar

Falling

Skylar was the 167th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,818 girls. It peaked in 2015, when about 1 in 337 girls got the name (5,295 babies). Its best-ever rank was #42 in 2015. Since 1880, 17% of babies named Skylar have been boys.
