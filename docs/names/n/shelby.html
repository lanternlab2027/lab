Shelby

Falling

Shelby was the 649th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 444 girls. It peaked in 1991, when about 1 in 183 girls got the name (10,220 babies). Its best-ever rank was #33 in 1991. Since 1880, 10% of babies named Shelby have been boys.
