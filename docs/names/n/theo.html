Theo

Rising

Theo was the 82nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 3,945 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 413 boys got the name (4,140 babies). Its best-ever rank was #78 in 2023. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Theo have been girls.
