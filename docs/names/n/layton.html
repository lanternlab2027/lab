Layton

Steady

Layton was the 714th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 377 boys. It peaked in 2015, when about 1 in 3,630 boys got the name (528 babies). Its best-ever rank was #541 in 2015. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Layton have been girls.
