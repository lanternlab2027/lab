Chris

Falling

Chris was the 646th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 431 boys. It peaked in 1961, when about 1 in 295 boys got the name (7,203 babies). Its best-ever rank was #55 in 1966. Since 1880, 13% of babies named Chris have been girls.
