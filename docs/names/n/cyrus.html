Cyrus

Vintage revival

Cyrus was the 268th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,284 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 1,240 boys got the name (1,389 babies). Its best-ever rank was #216 in 1880.
