London

Falling

London was the 431st most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 719 girls. It peaked in 2013, when about 1 in 507 girls got the name (3,462 babies). Its best-ever rank was #85 in 2013. Since 1880, 19% of babies named London have been boys.
