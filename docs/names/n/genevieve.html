Genevieve

Vintage revival

Genevieve was the 148th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,981 girls. It peaked in 1915, when about 1 in 336 girls got the name (2,928 babies). Its best-ever rank was #76 in 1914.
