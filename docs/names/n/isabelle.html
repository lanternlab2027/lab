Isabelle

Vintage revival

Isabelle was the 166th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,825 girls. It peaked in 2007, when about 1 in 468 girls got the name (4,106 babies). Its best-ever rank was #79 in 2007.
