Hayden

Falling

Hayden was the 161st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,268 boys. It peaked in 2006, when about 1 in 318 boys got the name (6,470 babies). Its best-ever rank was #71 in 2007. Since 1880, 23% of babies named Hayden have been girls.
