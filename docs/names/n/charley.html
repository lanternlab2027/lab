Charley

Falling

Charley was the 760th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 356 girls. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 2,787 girls got the name (641 babies). Its best-ever rank was #489 in 2014. Since 1880, 68% of babies named Charley have been boys.
