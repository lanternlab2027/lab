Ellis

Vintage revival

Ellis was the 243rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,417 boys. It peaked in 1899, when about 1 in 1,084 boys got the name (98 babies). Its best-ever rank was #155 in 1899. Since 1880, 13% of babies named Ellis have been girls.
