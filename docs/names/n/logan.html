Logan

Falling

Logan was the 53rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 5,576 boys. It peaked in 2017, when about 1 in 132 boys got the name (14,061 babies). Its best-ever rank was #5 in 2017. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Logan have been girls.
