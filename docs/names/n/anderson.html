Anderson

Vintage revival

Anderson was the 363rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 898 boys. It peaked in 2012, when about 1 in 1,519 boys got the name (1,249 babies). Its best-ever rank was #215 in 1882.
