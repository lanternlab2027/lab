Elizabeth

Classic

Elizabeth was the 17th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 6,760 girls. It peaked in 1880, when about 1 in 47 girls got the name (1,939 babies). Its best-ever rank was #3 in 1887.
