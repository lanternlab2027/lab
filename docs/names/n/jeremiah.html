Jeremiah

Vintage revival

Jeremiah was the 88th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 3,840 boys. It peaked in 2010, when about 1 in 248 boys got the name (7,743 babies). Its best-ever rank was #49 in 2011.
