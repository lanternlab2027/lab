Tommy

Falling

Tommy was the 658th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 420 boys. It peaked in 1944, when about 1 in 369 boys got the name (3,696 babies). Its best-ever rank was #70 in 1944.

Trend: Falling. Long-term faller: its share of births is down 91% from its peak decade (the 1940s), though it has held about level over the last ten years.
