Emerald

Rising

Emerald was the 741st most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 367 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 4,081 girls got the name (397 babies). Its best-ever rank was #708 in 2024. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Emerald have been boys.
