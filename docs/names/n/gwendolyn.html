Gwendolyn

Vintage revival

Gwendolyn was the 360th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 848 girls. It peaked in 1953, when about 1 in 535 girls got the name (3,519 babies). Its best-ever rank was #112 in 1951.
