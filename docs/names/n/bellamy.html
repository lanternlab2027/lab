Bellamy

Rising

Bellamy was the 721st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 374 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 3,914 boys got the name (447 babies). Its best-ever rank was #621 in 2021. Since 1880, 55% of babies named Bellamy have been girls.
