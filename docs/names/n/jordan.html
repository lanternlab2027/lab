Jordan

Falling

Jordan was the 131st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,732 boys. It peaked in 1991, when about 1 in 126 boys got the name (16,020 babies). Its best-ever rank was #26 in 1997. Since 1880, 25% of babies named Jordan have been girls.
