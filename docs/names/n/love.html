Love

Rising

Love was the 957th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 269 girls. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 4,179 girls got the name (384 babies). Its best-ever rank was #732 in 2023. Since 1880, 17% of babies named Love have been boys.
