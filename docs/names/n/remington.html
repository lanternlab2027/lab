Remington

Rising

Remington was the 307th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,105 boys. It peaked in 2020, when about 1 in 1,063 boys got the name (1,624 babies). Its best-ever rank was #227 in 2019. Since 1880, 30% of babies named Remington have been girls.
