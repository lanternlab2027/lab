Devin

Falling

Devin was the 548th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 548 boys. It peaked in 1998, when about 1 in 307 boys got the name (6,218 babies). Its best-ever rank was #58 in 1995. Since 1880, 10% of babies named Devin have been girls.
