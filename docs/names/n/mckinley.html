Mckinley

Falling

Mckinley was the 714th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 393 girls. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 2,072 girls got the name (862 babies). Its best-ever rank was #379 in 2014. Since 1880, 43% of babies named Mckinley have been boys.
