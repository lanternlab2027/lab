Alexis

Falling

Alexis was the 513th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 584 boys. It peaked in 2004, when about 1 in 475 boys got the name (4,175 babies). Its best-ever rank was #103 in 2004. Since 1880, 84% of babies named Alexis have been girls.
