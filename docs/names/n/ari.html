Ari

Rising

Ari was the 393rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 808 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 1,695 boys got the name (1,030 babies). Its best-ever rank was #328 in 2022. Since 1880, 27% of babies named Ari have been girls.
