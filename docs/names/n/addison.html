Addison

Falling

Addison was the 84th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,971 girls. It peaked in 2007, when about 1 in 161 girls got the name (11,949 babies). Its best-ever rank was #11 in 2007. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Addison have been boys.
