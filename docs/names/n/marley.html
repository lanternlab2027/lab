Marley

Steady

Marley was the 322nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 950 girls. It peaked in 2008, when about 1 in 802 girls got the name (2,359 babies). Its best-ever rank was #145 in 2008. Since 1880, 14% of babies named Marley have been boys.
