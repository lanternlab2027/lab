Ophelia

Vintage revival

Ophelia was the 264th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,183 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 1,325 girls got the name (1,223 babies). Its best-ever rank was #232 in 1882.
