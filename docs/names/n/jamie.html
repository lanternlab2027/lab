Jamie

Rising

Jamie was the 577th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 508 boys. It peaked in 1976, when about 1 in 340 boys got the name (4,621 babies). Its best-ever rank was #62 in 1975. Since 1880, 75% of babies named Jamie have been girls.
