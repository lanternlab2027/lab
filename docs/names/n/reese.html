Reese

Steady

Reese was the 192nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,631 girls. It peaked in 2012, when about 1 in 716 girls got the name (2,459 babies). Its best-ever rank was #128 in 2012. Since 1880, 30% of babies named Reese have been boys.
