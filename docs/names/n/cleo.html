Cleo

Vintage revival

Cleo was the 577th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 516 girls. It peaked in 1907, when about 1 in 980 girls got the name (325 babies). Its best-ever rank was #171 in 1907. Since 1880, 26% of babies named Cleo have been boys.
