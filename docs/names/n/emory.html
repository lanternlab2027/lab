Emory

Rising

Emory was the 339th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 899 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 1,724 girls got the name (940 babies). Its best-ever rank was #333 in 2024. Since 1880, 64% of babies named Emory have been boys.
