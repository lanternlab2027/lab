Flynn

Steady

Flynn was the 828th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 299 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 4,185 boys got the name (418 babies). Its best-ever rank was #654 in 2021. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Flynn have been girls.
