Rylan

Falling

Rylan was the 416th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 771 boys. It peaked in 2011, when about 1 in 687 boys got the name (2,766 babies). Its best-ever rank was #146 in 2011. Since 1880, 16% of babies named Rylan have been girls.
