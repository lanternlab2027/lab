Sunny

Rising

Sunny was the 390th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 798 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 1,915 girls got the name (846 babies). Its best-ever rank was #370 in 2024. Since 1880, 25% of babies named Sunny have been boys.
