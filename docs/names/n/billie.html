Billie

Vintage revival

Billie was the 691st most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 409 girls. It peaked in 1930, when about 1 in 347 girls got the name (3,241 babies). Its best-ever rank was #79 in 1929. Since 1880, 23% of babies named Billie have been boys.
