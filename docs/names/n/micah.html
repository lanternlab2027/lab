Micah

Steady

Micah was the 90th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 3,817 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 439 boys got the name (3,897 babies). Its best-ever rank was #86 in 2024. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Micah have been girls.
