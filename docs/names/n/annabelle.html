Annabelle

Vintage revival

Annabelle was the 343rd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 891 girls. It peaked in 2014, when about 1 in 409 girls got the name (4,363 babies). Its best-ever rank was #57 in 2014.
