Blake

Falling

Blake was the 316th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,080 boys. It peaked in 2012, when about 1 in 314 boys got the name (6,047 babies). Its best-ever rank was #71 in 2012. Since 1880, 10% of babies named Blake have been girls.
