Leslie

Falling

Leslie was the 712th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 393 girls. It peaked in 1969, when about 1 in 289 girls got the name (5,836 babies). Its best-ever rank was #56 in 1981. Since 1880, 29% of babies named Leslie have been boys.
