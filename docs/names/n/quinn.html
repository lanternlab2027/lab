Quinn

Rising

Quinn was the 97th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,634 girls. It peaked in 2017, when about 1 in 480 girls got the name (3,597 babies). Its best-ever rank was #73 in 2022. Since 1880, 41% of babies named Quinn have been boys.
