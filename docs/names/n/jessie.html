Jessie

Falling

Jessie was the 824th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 330 girls. It peaked in 1882, when about 1 in 134 girls got the name (806 babies). Its best-ever rank was #35 in 1881. Since 1880, 40% of babies named Jessie have been boys.
