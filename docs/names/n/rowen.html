Rowen

Rising

Rowen was the 488th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 617 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 2,430 boys got the name (709 babies). Its best-ever rank was #445 in 2024. Since 1880, 24% of babies named Rowen have been girls.
