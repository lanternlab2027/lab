Callen

Rising

Callen was the 516th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 579 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 2,490 boys got the name (687 babies). Its best-ever rank was #450 in 2023. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Callen have been girls.
