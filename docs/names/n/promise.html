Promise

Rising

Promise was the 826th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 330 girls. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 4,062 girls got the name (395 babies). Its best-ever rank was #714 in 2023. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Promise have been boys.
