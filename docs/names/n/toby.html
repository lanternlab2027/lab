Toby

Falling

Toby was the 979th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 234 boys. It peaked in 1975, when about 1 in 1,311 boys got the name (1,191 babies). Its best-ever rank was #190 in 1975. Since 1880, 19% of babies named Toby have been girls.
