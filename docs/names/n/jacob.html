Jacob

Vintage revival

Jacob was the 43rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 6,152 boys. It peaked in 1998, when about 1 in 53 boys got the name (36,031 babies). Its best-ever rank was #1 in 1999.
