Mary

Falling

Mary was the 125th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,230 girls. It peaked in 1880, when about 1 in 13 girls got the name (7,065 babies). Its best-ever rank was #1 in 1880.

Trend: Falling. Long-term faller: its share of births is down 98% from its peak decade (the 1880s), though it has held about level over the last ten years.
