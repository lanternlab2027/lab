Wren

Rising

Wren was the 231st most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,331 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 1,019 girls got the name (1,609 babies). Its best-ever rank was #184 in 2022. Since 1880, 14% of babies named Wren have been boys.
