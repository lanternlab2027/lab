Angel

Steady

Angel was the 67th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 4,757 boys. It peaked in 2006, when about 1 in 177 boys got the name (11,602 babies). Its best-ever rank was #31 in 2006. Since 1880, 27% of babies named Angel have been girls.
