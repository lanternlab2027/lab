Remy

Rising

Remy was the 408th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 781 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 1,890 boys got the name (924 babies). Its best-ever rank was #356 in 2020. Since 1880, 40% of babies named Remy have been girls.
