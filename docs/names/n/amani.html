Amani

Steady

Amani was the 678th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 419 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 3,197 girls got the name (513 babies). Its best-ever rank was #553 in 2010. Since 1880, 18% of babies named Amani have been boys.
