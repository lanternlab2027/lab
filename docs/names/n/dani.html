Dani

Rising

Dani was the 747th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 364 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 3,797 girls got the name (432 babies). Its best-ever rank was #674 in 2022. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Dani have been boys.
