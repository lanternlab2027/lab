Arya

Rising

Arya was the 158th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,858 girls. It peaked in 2019, when about 1 in 550 girls got the name (3,051 babies). Its best-ever rank was #92 in 2019. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Arya have been boys.
