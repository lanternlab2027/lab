Jamari

Steady

Jamari was the 576th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 509 boys. It peaked in 2010, when about 1 in 2,589 boys got the name (741 babies). Its best-ever rank was #386 in 2010. Since 1880, 6% of babies named Jamari have been girls.
