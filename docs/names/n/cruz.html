Cruz

Steady

Cruz was the 331st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,025 boys. It peaked in 2013, when about 1 in 1,513 boys got the name (1,250 babies). Its best-ever rank was #279 in 2013. Since 1880, 12% of babies named Cruz have been girls.
