Kyrie

Rising

Kyrie was the 244th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,416 boys. It peaked in 2021, when about 1 in 1,016 boys got the name (1,722 babies). Its best-ever rank was #215 in 2017. Since 1880, 19% of babies named Kyrie have been girls.
