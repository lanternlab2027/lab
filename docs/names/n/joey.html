Joey

Falling

Joey was the 877th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 274 boys. It peaked in 1976, when about 1 in 1,353 boys got the name (1,160 babies). Its best-ever rank was #194 in 1976. Since 1880, 12% of babies named Joey have been girls.
