Saige

Rising

Saige was the 480th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 640 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 2,073 girls got the name (791 babies). Its best-ever rank was #395 in 2022. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Saige have been boys.
