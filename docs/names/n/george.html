George

Falling

George was the 126th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,848 boys. It peaked in 1880, when about 1 in 22 boys got the name (5,126 babies). Its best-ever rank was #4 in 1881.

Trend: Falling. Long-term faller: its share of births is down 96% from its peak decade (the 1880s), though it has held about level over the last ten years.
