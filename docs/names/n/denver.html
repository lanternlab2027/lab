Denver

Rising

Denver was the 518th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 578 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 2,707 boys got the name (645 babies). Its best-ever rank was #422 in 1919. Since 1880, 18% of babies named Denver have been girls.
