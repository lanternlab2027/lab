Jaime

Falling

Jaime was the 657th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 420 boys. It peaked in 1976, when about 1 in 1,119 boys got the name (1,402 babies). Its best-ever rank was #167 in 1980. Since 1880, 41% of babies named Jaime have been girls.
