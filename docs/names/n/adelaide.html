Adelaide

Vintage revival

Adelaide was the 289th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,062 girls. It peaked in 1884, when about 1 in 1,317 girls got the name (98 babies). Its best-ever rank was #179 in 1883.
