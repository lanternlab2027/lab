Harlow

Rising

Harlow was the 309th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 981 girls. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 1,260 girls got the name (1,273 babies). Its best-ever rank was #237 in 2021. Since 1880, 16% of babies named Harlow have been boys.
