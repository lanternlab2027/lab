Jade

Steady

Jade was the 95th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,658 girls. It peaked in 2002, when about 1 in 495 girls got the name (3,630 babies). Its best-ever rank was #80 in 2023. Since 1880, 5% of babies named Jade have been boys.
