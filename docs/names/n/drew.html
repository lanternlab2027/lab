Drew

Falling

Drew was the 553rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 541 boys. It peaked in 1985, when about 1 in 812 boys got the name (2,276 babies). Its best-ever rank was #126 in 1985. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Drew have been girls.
