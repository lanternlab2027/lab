Landry

Steady

Landry was the 935th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 279 girls. It peaked in 2018, when about 1 in 4,846 girls got the name (351 babies). Its best-ever rank was #778 in 2018. Since 1880, 50% of babies named Landry have been boys.
