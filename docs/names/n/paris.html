Paris

Falling

Paris was the 592nd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 497 girls. It peaked in 2004, when about 1 in 854 girls got the name (2,150 babies). Its best-ever rank was #157 in 2004. Since 1880, 22% of babies named Paris have been boys.
