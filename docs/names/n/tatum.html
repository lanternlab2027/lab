Tatum

Rising

Tatum was the 202nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,736 boys. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 936 boys got the name (1,841 babies). Its best-ever rank was #195 in 2024. Since 1880, 68% of babies named Tatum have been girls.
