Asa

Vintage revival

Asa was the 483rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 622 boys. It peaked in 1881, when about 1 in 1,550 boys got the name (65 babies). Its best-ever rank was #178 in 1881. Since 1880, 8% of babies named Asa have been girls.
