Louie

Vintage revival

Louie was the 596th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 481 boys. It peaked in 1901, when about 1 in 1,836 boys got the name (58 babies). Its best-ever rank was #230 in 1901. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Louie have been girls.
