Keegan

Falling

Keegan was the 621st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 449 boys. It peaked in 2008, when about 1 in 1,212 boys got the name (1,683 babies). Its best-ever rank was #222 in 2007. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Keegan have been girls.
