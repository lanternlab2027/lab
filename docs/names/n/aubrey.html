Aubrey

Falling

Aubrey was the 146th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,003 girls. It peaked in 2012, when about 1 in 218 girls got the name (8,061 babies). Its best-ever rank was #15 in 2012. Since 1880, 19% of babies named Aubrey have been boys.
