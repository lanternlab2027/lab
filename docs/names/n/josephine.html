Josephine

Vintage revival

Josephine was the 53rd most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 4,126 girls. It peaked in 1916, when about 1 in 129 girls got the name (8,127 babies). Its best-ever rank was #21 in 1916.
