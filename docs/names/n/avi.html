Avi

Rising

Avi was the 832nd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 297 boys. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 4,771 boys got the name (366 babies). Its best-ever rank was #720 in 2022. Since 1880, 12% of babies named Avi have been girls.
