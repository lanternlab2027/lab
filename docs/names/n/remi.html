Remi

Rising

Remi was the 145th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 2,023 girls. It peaked in 2022, when about 1 in 686 girls got the name (2,392 babies). Its best-ever rank was #118 in 2022. Since 1880, 15% of babies named Remi have been boys.
