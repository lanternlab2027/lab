Kamari

Rising

Kamari was the 391st most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 811 boys. It peaked in 2023, when about 1 in 1,931 boys got the name (886 babies). Its best-ever rank was #366 in 2023. Since 1880, 32% of babies named Kamari have been girls.
