Karson

Steady

Karson was the 453rd most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 690 boys. It peaked in 2020, when about 1 in 1,339 boys got the name (1,289 babies). Its best-ever rank was #265 in 2018. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Karson have been girls.
