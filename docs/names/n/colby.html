Colby

Falling

Colby was the 615th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 455 boys. It peaked in 2001, when about 1 in 503 boys got the name (3,859 babies). Its best-ever rank was #99 in 2001. Since 1880, 7% of babies named Colby have been girls.
