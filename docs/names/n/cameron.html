Cameron

Falling

Cameron was the 76th most popular name for boys born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 4,348 boys. It peaked in 2000, when about 1 in 154 boys got the name (12,767 babies). Its best-ever rank was #31 in 2000. Since 1880, 9% of babies named Cameron have been girls.
