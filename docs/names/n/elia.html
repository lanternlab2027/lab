Elia

Rising

Elia was the 970th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 263 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 5,047 girls got the name (321 babies). Its best-ever rank was #841 in 2024. Since 1880, 11% of babies named Elia have been boys.
