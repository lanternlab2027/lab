Arden

Steady

Arden was the 975th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 261 girls. It peaked in 2015, when about 1 in 5,950 girls got the name (300 babies). Its best-ever rank was #738 in 1930. Since 1880, 53% of babies named Arden have been boys.
