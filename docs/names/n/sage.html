Sage

Rising

Sage was the 160th most popular name for girls born in the U.S. in 2025, given to 1,853 girls. It peaked in 2024, when about 1 in 813 girls got the name (1,994 babies). Its best-ever rank was #142 in 2023. Since 1880, 34% of babies named Sage have been boys.
