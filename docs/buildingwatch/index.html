The problem

Nobody pings you

DOB and HPD post new violations and complaints to their public databases. DOB's free email updates cover construction job status, not new violations or complaints. Paper notices go to the address on file, and that mail gets missed.

Missed hearings cost more

An ECB/OATH summons has a hearing date. If no one responds, OATH can decide the case by default, which can mean a higher penalty than if you had answered.

Deadlines hide in the fine print

HPD violations carry correct-by and certify-by dates. Annual filings like the bedbug report and property registration recur every year.
