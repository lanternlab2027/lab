The 2027 NYC compliance calendar for small building owners

Every city deadline that owners of 1-20 unit buildings face in 2027, month by month, on printable letter-size pages. Each date was checked on nyc.gov, and each item lists its source.

What's inside (17 pages)

  • HPD registration, bedbug filing, and the January window-guard, lead and stove knob notices
  • New for 2027: natural gas detectors required by Jan 1
  • Gas piping (Local Law 152) and boiler inspection cycles, plus facade (FISP) dates
  • Heat season, property tax due dates, and the tax appeal deadlines
  • LL84, LL97 and energy grade dates for larger buildings, plus RPIE
  • Tags showing which buildings each item applies to (3+ units, built before 1960, rent-stabilized and so on)
  • A year-at-a-glance page, a standing-rules page, and a full source list
