How to export your library

Both sites give you a single .csv file. Upload it here as it is; there's no need to open or edit it first.

Goodreads (on a computer)
  1. Sign in at goodreads.com in a desktop browser. The phone app has no export, but "Request desktop site" in your phone's browser works.
  2. Click My Books at the top.
  3. In the left sidebar, under Tools, click Import and export.
  4. Click Export Library and wait a moment for the file to be prepared.
  5. Click the download link that appears, then upload goodreads_library_export.csv here.
StoryGraph
  1. Sign in at thestorygraph.com and open your profile menu.
  2. Go to Manage Account and scroll down to the section for exporting your data.
  3. Choose the option to export your StoryGraph library.
  4. When the file is ready (StoryGraph may email you a link), download the .csv and upload it here.
Why are some cards missing?

Cards only appear when your export has the data for them. StoryGraph exports don't include page counts or community ratings, so the pages, longest-book and "harsh critic" cards are Goodreads-only. StoryGraph users get a mood mix and a ratings breakdown instead. The abandoned-book card needs a DNF shelf (for Goodreads, an exclusive shelf named something like dnf or did-not-finish).

Your library never leaves your device. How we know:

There's no server to send it to

This page is plain files on GitHub Pages. Your export is read, counted and drawn into cards by JavaScript running in this browser tab. Close the tab and it's gone.

The browser blocks it anyway

The page ships a Content-Security-Policy that only lets it talk to one outside address: the optional email signup below, which sends just your email. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.

It's open source

Every line is on GitHub: lanternlab2027/lab → docs/reading. The zip helper (JSZip) is copied into the folder, not loaded from a CDN.

Try it offline

Load this page, turn on airplane mode, then upload your export. It still works, because nothing needs the internet.

Questions

Is this made by Goodreads or StoryGraph?

No. Reading Wrapped is an independent project that works with the export files those sites already let you download. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Goodreads, Amazon or The StoryGraph.

Which books count toward a year?

Books on your read shelf whose finish date (Goodreads Date Read, StoryGraph Dates Read or Last Date Read) falls in that year. If some books are marked read with no date, you can tick the box above the cards to count them by the date you added them instead.

How is the reader personality picked?

From your numbers alone: average book length, how many books you read, how loyal you are to one author, how many genres you cover, how your ratings compare with everyone else's, how often you re-read, and how bunched-up your reading is across the year. The strongest signal wins.

It says "doesn't look like a Goodreads or StoryGraph export"

Upload the original .csv from the export page. Files re-saved from Excel or Numbers usually still work, but a file from another app (or a spreadsheet with renamed columns) won't be recognized.

Is it free?

Yes, completely.
