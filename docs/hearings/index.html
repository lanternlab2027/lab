What to do before your hearing

Plain-English summary of OATH's own instructions. Rules can change, so follow the official page linked in each step.

  1. Check the date on your most recent notice. If the date we show differs from your summons, OATH says to go by the most recent notice you received. Questions: call OATH at 844-OATH-NYC (844-628-4692). OATH hearing FAQ
  2. Decide: pay or fight. Paying before the hearing date means you admit the charge and give up your right to a hearing. Payments and penalties
  3. Fight it online, with no trip. Many summonses qualify for an Online Hearing: a form where you type your defense and attach up to three files. It must be submitted on or before the hearing date. Not every violation code is eligible. Online Hearing
  4. Or have your hearing by phone. Request it at least 3 business days, and no more than 1 month, before the hearing date. OATH emails you a number and PIN; on the day you call in within three hours of the hearing time. Hearing by phone · request form
  5. In person or by mail are options too. In-person hearings must be requested by email at least 5 business days ahead. A mailed written defense must reach OATH before the hearing date. In person · By mail
  6. Can't make the date? Reschedule before it. The reschedule request must reach OATH before the scheduled hearing. Reschedule a hearing
  7. Already missed it? Act within 75 days. OATH grants a request for a new hearing if it is received within 75 days of the missed hearing date, and you can ask only once per summons. Missed hearing (default) FAQ · request online
