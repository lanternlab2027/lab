How to get your data

Both apps let you download a copy of your own data. It arrives by email as a zip. Upload the zip here as it is, or just the one file the cards need.

Tinder: Download My Information
  1. Open Tinder’s Manage My Account tool (linked from the help article below) and sign in.
  2. Tap Download My Information.
  3. Enter the email address where you want the link, then tap Submit.
  4. Tinder emails a download link, usually within a few days. The link expires 24 hours after it’s sent, so download it promptly (you can request again if you miss it).
  5. Upload the zip here. Only data.json inside it is read; photos and the rest are skipped.

Steps from Tinder’s help center: How do I request a copy of my personal data?

Hinge: Download My Data
  1. In the Hinge app, tap your photo icon at the far right of the bottom bar, then Settings.
  2. Scroll down to the option to download your data.
  3. Pick your country, confirm the request, then verify your email address.
  4. Hinge emails you when it’s ready. Most exports are ready within 30 days, and the download lasts 48 hours.
  5. Upload the zip here. Older exports have matches.json; newer ones have .csv tables such as match_likes.csv and chat_message.csv. Both work.

What the cards read, and what they never touch

Read and counted: daily totals of app opens, likes, passes, super likes, matches and messages (Tinder’s Usage section), plus the times of your likes, skips, matches and sent messages (Hinge, and Tinder’s message times). Each message is counted, never read.

Never shown or kept: message text, matches’ names, photos, prompts, comments, your profile, location, email or phone. Hinge’s profile and media files are skipped entirely.

Limits: Tinder’s daily totals have no times, so the clock card uses the times you sent messages. Newer Hinge exports have no match list, so the match-rate card only appears for Tinder and older Hinge exports. Times are shown in your device’s time zone.

Your data never leaves your device. How we know:

There’s no server to send it to

This page is plain files on GitHub Pages. Your export is read, counted and drawn into cards by JavaScript running in this browser tab. Close the tab and it’s gone.

The browser blocks it anyway

The page ships a Content-Security-Policy that only lets it talk to two outside addresses: the optional email signup below, which sends just your email, and our page-view counter, which never sees your file. We count anonymous page views with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal data). No ad trackers, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.

It’s open source

Every line is on GitHub: lanternlab2027/lab → docs/dating. The zip helper (JSZip) is copied into the folder, not loaded from a CDN.

Try it offline

Load this page, turn on airplane mode, then upload your export. It still works, because nothing needs the internet.

Email me when to come back

Optional. Exports take days to arrive, so we’ll send one reminder, plus a note when next year’s edition is ready. We only send your email address; nothing from your export is ever included.

Questions

Do Tinder or Hinge have their own Wrapped?

Not a personal year in review you can share. This builds one from the data export both apps already let you download, so it’s a Tinder Wrapped and Hinge Wrapped in one place.

Is this made by Tinder or Hinge?

No. Dating Wrapped is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Tinder, Hinge or Match Group, and uses no app logos or artwork.

Will my matches see anything about them?

No. Cards only show your own totals and percentages. No names, messages, photos or details of any other person ever appear, and nothing is posted unless you share a card yourself.

What’s “Safe share”?

It’s on by default. Cards then show rates, ratios, dates and labels (“38% right swipes”, “9× your usual day”) instead of totals like how many swipes or matches you had. Turn it off if you’re happy to share the exact numbers.

How is the dating personality picked?

From your numbers alone: how often you swipe right, your match rate, what hour you’re most active, your busiest day, your streak, your peak month and how often you send the first message. The strongest signal wins. It’s just for fun.

I use both apps. Can I combine them?

Yes. Upload one export, then tap “Add your other app’s export”. You can show both together or one at a time.

It says “doesn’t look like a Tinder or Hinge data export”

Upload the zip the app emailed you, or the file inside it: data.json for Tinder, matches.json or the .csv files for Hinge. Files from other apps aren’t supported yet.

Is it free?

Yes, completely.
