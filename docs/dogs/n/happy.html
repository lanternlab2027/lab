There are 245 licensed dogs named Happy in NYC.

Happy is the 198th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 245 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,357. 40% female, 60% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
