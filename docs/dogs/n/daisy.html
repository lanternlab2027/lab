Daisy

Steady

There are 1,699 licensed dogs named Daisy in NYC.

Daisy is the 11th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,699 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 196. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
