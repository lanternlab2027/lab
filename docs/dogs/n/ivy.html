Ivy

Rising

There are 188 licensed dogs named Ivy in NYC.

Ivy is the 282nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 188 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,769. 98% are female. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
