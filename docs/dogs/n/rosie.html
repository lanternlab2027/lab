Rosie

Rising

There are 978 licensed dogs named Rosie in NYC.

Rosie is the 32nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 978 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 340. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
