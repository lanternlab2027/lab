There are 272 licensed dogs named Casper in NYC.

Casper is the 173rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 272 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,223. 97% are male. The most common breed is Maltese. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is fading.
