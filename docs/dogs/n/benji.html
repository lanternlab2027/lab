Benji

There are 865 licensed dogs named Benji in NYC.

Benji is the 35th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 865 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 384. 99% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
