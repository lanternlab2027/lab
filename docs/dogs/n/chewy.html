There are 397 licensed dogs named Chewy in NYC.

Chewy is the 119th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 397 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 838. 13% female, 87% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is steady.
