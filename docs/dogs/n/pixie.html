Pixie

Rising

There are 133 licensed dogs named Pixie in NYC.

Pixie is the 419th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 133 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,500. 98% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
