Not

There are 620 licensed dogs named Not in NYC.

Not is the 61st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 620 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 536. 33% female, 67% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
