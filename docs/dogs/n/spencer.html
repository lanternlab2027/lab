Spencer

Fading

There are 132 licensed dogs named Spencer in NYC.

Spencer is the 421st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 132 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,519. 92% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
