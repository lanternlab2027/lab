There are 207 licensed dogs named Roscoe in NYC.

Roscoe is the 250th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 207 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,606. 99% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
