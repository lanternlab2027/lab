There are 805 licensed dogs named Zeus in NYC.

Zeus is the 42nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 805 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 413. Over 99% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
