Oreo

There are 1,151 licensed dogs named Oreo in NYC.

Oreo is the 21st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,151 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 289. 28% female, 72% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
