Pepper

Rising

There are 848 licensed dogs named Pepper in NYC.

Pepper is the 36th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 848 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 392. 78% female, 22% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
