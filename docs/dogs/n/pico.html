There are 37 licensed dogs named Pico in NYC.

Pico is the 1347th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 37 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 8,988. 16% female, 84% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
