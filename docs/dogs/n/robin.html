Robin

There are 59 licensed dogs named Robin in NYC.

Robin is the 905th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 59 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 5,636. 44% female, 56% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
