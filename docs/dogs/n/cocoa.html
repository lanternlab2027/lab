There are 264 licensed dogs named Cocoa in NYC.

Cocoa is the 180th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 264 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,260. 75% female, 25% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
