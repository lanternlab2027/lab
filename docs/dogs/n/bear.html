Bear

Steady

There are 868 licensed dogs named Bear in NYC.

Bear is the 34th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 868 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 383. 91% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
