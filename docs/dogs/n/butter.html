Butter

There are 142 licensed dogs named Butter in NYC.

Butter is the 390th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 142 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,342. 45% female, 55% male. The most common breed is Golden Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
