There are 104 licensed dogs named Mack in NYC.

Mack is the 546th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 104 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,197. 97% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
