Luke

Steady

There are 267 licensed dogs named Luke in NYC.

Luke is the 176th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 267 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,245. Over 99% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is steady.
