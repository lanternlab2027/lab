Diamond

Fading

There are 394 licensed dogs named Diamond in NYC.

Diamond is the 122nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 394 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 844. 93% are female. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
