Winnie

Rising

There are 507 licensed dogs named Winnie in NYC.

Winnie is the 82nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 507 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 656. 92% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
