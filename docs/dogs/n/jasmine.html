There are 140 licensed dogs named Jasmine in NYC.

Jasmine is the 397th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 140 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,375. 100% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
