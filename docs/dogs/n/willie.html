Willie

There are 146 licensed dogs named Willie in NYC.

Willie is the 374th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 146 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,278. 93% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
