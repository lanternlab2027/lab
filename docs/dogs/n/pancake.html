There are 70 licensed dogs named Pancake in NYC.

Pancake is the 787th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 70 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,751. 64% female, 36% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
