Dream

Rising

There are 50 licensed dogs named Dream in NYC.

Dream is the 1065th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 50 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,651. 78% female, 22% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
