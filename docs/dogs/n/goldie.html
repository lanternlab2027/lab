Goldie

There are 155 licensed dogs named Goldie in NYC.

Goldie is the 344th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 155 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,145. 89% female, 11% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
