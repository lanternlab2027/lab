Riley

Steady

There are 821 licensed dogs named Riley in NYC.

Riley is the 38th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 821 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 405. 51% female, 49% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
