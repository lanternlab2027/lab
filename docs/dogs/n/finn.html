Finn

Rising

There are 427 licensed dogs named Finn in NYC.

Finn is the 110th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 427 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 779. 97% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
