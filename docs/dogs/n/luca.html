Luca

Rising

There are 309 licensed dogs named Luca in NYC.

Luca is the 153rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 309 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,076. 94% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
