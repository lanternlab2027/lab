There are 126 licensed dogs named Bagel in NYC.

Bagel is the 443rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 126 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,639. 27% female, 73% male. The most common breed is Beagle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
