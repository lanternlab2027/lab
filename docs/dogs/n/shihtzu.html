Shihtzu

Rising

There are 91 licensed dogs named Shihtzu in NYC.

Shihtzu is the 610th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 91 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,654. 28% female, 72% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in the Bronx. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
