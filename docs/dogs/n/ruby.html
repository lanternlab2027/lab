Ruby

Steady

There are 1,030 licensed dogs named Ruby in NYC.

Ruby is the 26th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,030 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 323. 98% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
