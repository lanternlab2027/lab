Kai

Rising

There are 201 licensed dogs named Kai in NYC.

Kai is the 260th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 201 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,654. 17% female, 83% male. The most common breed is German Shepherd. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
