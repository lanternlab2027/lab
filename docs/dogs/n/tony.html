Tony

There are 188 licensed dogs named Tony in NYC.

Tony is the 282nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 188 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,769. 97% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
