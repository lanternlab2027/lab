Hank

Rising

There are 285 licensed dogs named Hank in NYC.

Hank is the 166th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 285 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,167. 98% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
