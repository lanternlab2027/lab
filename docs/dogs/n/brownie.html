Brownie

Steady

There are 610 licensed dogs named Brownie in NYC.

Brownie is the 64th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 610 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 545. 28% female, 72% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
