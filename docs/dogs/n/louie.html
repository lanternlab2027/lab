Louie

Steady

There are 794 licensed dogs named Louie in NYC.

Louie is the 44th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 794 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 419. 97% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
