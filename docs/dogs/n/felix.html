Felix

Steady

There are 114 licensed dogs named Felix in NYC.

Felix is the 492nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 114 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,917. 98% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is steady.
