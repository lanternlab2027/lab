There are 457 licensed dogs named Lexi in NYC.

Lexi is the 98th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 457 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 728. 99% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
