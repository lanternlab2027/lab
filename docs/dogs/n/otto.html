Otto

There are 259 licensed dogs named Otto in NYC.

Otto is the 184th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 259 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,284. 98% are male. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is steady.
