Ella

Steady

There are 420 licensed dogs named Ella in NYC.

Ella is the 114th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 420 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 792. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
