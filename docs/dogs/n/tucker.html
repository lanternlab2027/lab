Tucker

Fading

There are 389 licensed dogs named Tucker in NYC.

Tucker is the 123rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 389 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 855. 98% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
