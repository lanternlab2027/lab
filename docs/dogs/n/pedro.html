There are 45 licensed dogs named Pedro in NYC.

Pedro is the 1164th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 45 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,390. 98% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
