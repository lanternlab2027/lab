There are 373 licensed dogs named Moose in NYC.

Moose is the 130th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 373 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 892. 13% female, 87% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
