Echo

Rising

There are 111 licensed dogs named Echo in NYC.

Echo is the 506th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 111 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,996. 42% female, 58% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
