Harley

Steady

There are 613 licensed dogs named Harley in NYC.

Harley is the 62nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 613 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 542. 58% female, 42% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is steady.
