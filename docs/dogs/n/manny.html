There are 75 licensed dogs named Manny in NYC.

Manny is the 734th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 75 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,434. 96% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is steady.
