There are 354 licensed dogs named Maximus in NYC.

Maximus is the 138th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 354 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 939. 99% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
