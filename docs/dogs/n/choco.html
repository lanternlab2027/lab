There are 116 licensed dogs named Choco in NYC.

Choco is the 484th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 116 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,867. 22% female, 78% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
