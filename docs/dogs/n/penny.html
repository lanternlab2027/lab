Penny

Steady

There are 1,225 licensed dogs named Penny in NYC.

Penny is the 19th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,225 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 271. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
