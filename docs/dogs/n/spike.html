There are 263 licensed dogs named Spike in NYC.

Spike is the 181st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 263 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,264. 98% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
