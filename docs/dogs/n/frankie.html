Frankie

Rising

There are 719 licensed dogs named Frankie in NYC.

Frankie is the 50th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 719 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 463. 41% female, 59% male. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
