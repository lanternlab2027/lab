There are 135 licensed dogs named Quinn in NYC.

Quinn is the 413th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 135 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,463. 66% female, 34% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
