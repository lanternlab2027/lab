Pickles

Rising

There are 121 licensed dogs named Pickles in NYC.

Pickles is the 463rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 121 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,748. 36% female, 64% male. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
