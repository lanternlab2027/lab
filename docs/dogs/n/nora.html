There are 81 licensed dogs named Nora in NYC.

Nora is the 688th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 81 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,105. 99% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
