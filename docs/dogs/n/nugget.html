There are 191 licensed dogs named Nugget in NYC.

Nugget is the 276th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 191 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,741. 38% female, 62% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
