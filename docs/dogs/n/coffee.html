Coffee

Rising

There are 113 licensed dogs named Coffee in NYC.

Coffee is the 495th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 113 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,943. 42% female, 58% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
