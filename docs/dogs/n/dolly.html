Dolly

Rising

There are 252 licensed dogs named Dolly in NYC.

Dolly is the 189th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 252 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,320. 98% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
