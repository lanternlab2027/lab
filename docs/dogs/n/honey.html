Honey

Steady

There are 644 licensed dogs named Honey in NYC.

Honey is the 58th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 644 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 516. 92% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
