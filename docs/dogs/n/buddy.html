Buddy

Fading

There are 1,534 licensed dogs named Buddy in NYC.

Buddy is the 12th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,534 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 217. 99% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
