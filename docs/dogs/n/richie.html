There are 35 licensed dogs named Richie in NYC.

Richie is the 1412th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 35 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 9,501. 97% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is fading.
