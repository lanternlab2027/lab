Snow

Steady

There are 294 licensed dogs named Snow in NYC.

Snow is the 158th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 294 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,131. 60% female, 40% male. The most common breed is Maltese. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
