Dino

There are 160 licensed dogs named Dino in NYC.

Dino is the 331st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 160 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,078. 98% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
