There are 104 licensed dogs named Flash in NYC.

Flash is the 546th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 104 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,197. 93% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is rising.
