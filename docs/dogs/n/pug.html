There are 54 licensed dogs named Pug in NYC.

Pug is the 977th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 54 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,158. 40% female, 60% male. The most common breed is Pug. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
