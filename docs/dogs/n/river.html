River

Rising

There are 149 licensed dogs named River in NYC.

River is the 364th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 149 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,232. 38% female, 62% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
