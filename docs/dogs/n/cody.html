Cody

Fading

There are 629 licensed dogs named Cody in NYC.

Cody is the 59th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 629 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 529. 97% are male. The most common breed is Maltese. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
