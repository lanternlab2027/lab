Angel

Fading

There are 375 licensed dogs named Angel in NYC.

Angel is the 129th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 375 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 887. 69% female, 31% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
