Logan

Steady

There are 263 licensed dogs named Logan in NYC.

Logan is the 181st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 263 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,264. 95% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is steady.
