Louis

Steady

There are 251 licensed dogs named Louis in NYC.

Louis is the 190th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 251 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,325. 98% are male. The most common breed is French Bulldog. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
