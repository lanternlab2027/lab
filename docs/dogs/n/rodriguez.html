There are 72 licensed dogs named Rodriguez in NYC.

Rodriguez is the 765th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 72 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,619. 43% female, 57% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
