There are 54 licensed dogs named Cupid in NYC.

Cupid is the 977th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 54 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,158. 13% female, 87% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is fading.
