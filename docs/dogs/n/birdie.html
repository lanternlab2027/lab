Birdie

Rising

There are 148 licensed dogs named Birdie in NYC.

Birdie is the 368th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 148 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,247. 95% are female. The most common breed is Golden Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
