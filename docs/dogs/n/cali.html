Cali

Rising

There are 246 licensed dogs named Cali in NYC.

Cali is the 195th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 246 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,352. 93% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is rising.
