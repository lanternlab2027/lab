Ace

Rising

There are 586 licensed dogs named Ace in NYC.

Ace is the 73rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 586 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 567. 98% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
