Simon

Steady

There are 222 licensed dogs named Simon in NYC.

Simon is the 228th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 222 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,498. 98% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
