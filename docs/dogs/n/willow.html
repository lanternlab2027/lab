Willow

Rising

There are 436 licensed dogs named Willow in NYC.

Willow is the 105th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 436 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 763. 94% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
