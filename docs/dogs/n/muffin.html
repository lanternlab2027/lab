There are 170 licensed dogs named Muffin in NYC.

Muffin is the 308th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 170 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,956. 70% female, 30% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
