Roxy

Fading

There are 709 licensed dogs named Roxy in NYC.

Roxy is the 53rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 709 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 469. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
