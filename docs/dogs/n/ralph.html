There are 137 licensed dogs named Ralph in NYC.

Ralph is the 408th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 137 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,427. 98% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is steady.
