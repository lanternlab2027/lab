Molly

Fading

There are 1,044 licensed dogs named Molly in NYC.

Molly is the 24th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,044 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 319. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
