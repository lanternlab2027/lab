There are 35 licensed dogs named Dorothy in NYC.

Dorothy is the 1412th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 35 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 9,501. 97% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
