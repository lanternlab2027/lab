There are 143 licensed dogs named Clover in NYC.

Clover is the 384th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 143 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,325. 70% female, 30% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
