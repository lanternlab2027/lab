Pom

Rising

There are 81 licensed dogs named Pom in NYC.

Pom is the 688th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 81 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,105. 38% female, 63% male. The most common breed is Pomeranian. Most live in the Bronx. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
