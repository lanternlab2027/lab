Tina

Steady

There are 133 licensed dogs named Tina in NYC.

Tina is the 419th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 133 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,500. 98% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
