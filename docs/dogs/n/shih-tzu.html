There are 45 licensed dogs named Shih-Tzu in NYC.

Shih-Tzu is the 1164th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 45 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,390. 26% female, 74% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
