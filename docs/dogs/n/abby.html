There are 346 licensed dogs named Abby in NYC.

Abby is the 141st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 346 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 961. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
