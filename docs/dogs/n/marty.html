There are 139 licensed dogs named Marty in NYC.

Marty is the 401st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 139 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,392. 12% female, 88% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is steady.
