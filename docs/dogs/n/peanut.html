There are 607 licensed dogs named Peanut in NYC.

Peanut is the 67th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 607 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 548. 38% female, 62% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
