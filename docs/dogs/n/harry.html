Harry

There are 346 licensed dogs named Harry in NYC.

Harry is the 141st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 346 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 961. 98% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
