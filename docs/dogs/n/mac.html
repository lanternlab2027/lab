Mac

Steady

There are 170 licensed dogs named Mac in NYC.

Mac is the 308th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 170 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,956. 95% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is steady.
