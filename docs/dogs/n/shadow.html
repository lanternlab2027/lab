Shadow

There are 710 licensed dogs named Shadow in NYC.

Shadow is the 52nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 710 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 468. 21% female, 79% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
