There are 59 licensed dogs named Frances in NYC.

Frances is the 905th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 59 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 5,636. 95% are female. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
