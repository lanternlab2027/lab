Jax

There are 578 licensed dogs named Jax in NYC.

Jax is the 74th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 578 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 575. 97% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
