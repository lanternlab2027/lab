Louise

Rising

There are 92 licensed dogs named Louise in NYC.

Louise is the 605th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 92 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,615. 96% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
