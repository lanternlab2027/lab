There are 231 licensed dogs named Trixie in NYC.

Trixie is the 214th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 231 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,440. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
