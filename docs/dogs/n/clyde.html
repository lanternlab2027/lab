Clyde

Fading

There are 140 licensed dogs named Clyde in NYC.

Clyde is the 397th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 140 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,375. 98% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
