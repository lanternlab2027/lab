There are 131 licensed dogs named Nikki in NYC.

Nikki is the 424th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 131 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,538. 86% female, 14% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
