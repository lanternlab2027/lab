There are 181 licensed dogs named Arlo in NYC.

Arlo is the 293rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 181 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,837. 96% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
