There are 449 licensed dogs named Gus in NYC.

Gus is the 101st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 449 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 741. 98% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
