Sasha

There are 607 licensed dogs named Sasha in NYC.

Sasha is the 67th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 607 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 548. 96% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
