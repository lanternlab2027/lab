Eddie

Steady

There are 159 licensed dogs named Eddie in NYC.

Eddie is the 334th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 159 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,091. 96% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
