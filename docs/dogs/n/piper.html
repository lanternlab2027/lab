There are 377 licensed dogs named Piper in NYC.

Piper is the 128th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 377 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 882. 94% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
