Bailey

Steady

There are 1,470 licensed dogs named Bailey in NYC.

Bailey is the 14th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,470 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 226. 54% female, 46% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is steady.
