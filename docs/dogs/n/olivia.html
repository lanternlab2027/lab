Olivia

Steady

There are 214 licensed dogs named Olivia in NYC.

Olivia is the 236th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 214 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,554. 100% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
