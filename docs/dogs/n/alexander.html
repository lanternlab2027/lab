Alexander

There are 54 licensed dogs named Alexander in NYC.

Alexander is the 977th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 54 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,158. 93% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
