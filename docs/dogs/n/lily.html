Lily

Steady

There are 1,018 licensed dogs named Lily in NYC.

Lily is the 28th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,018 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 327. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
