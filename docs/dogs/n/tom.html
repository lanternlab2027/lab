There are 58 licensed dogs named Tom in NYC.

Tom is the 919th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 58 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 5,733. 98% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
