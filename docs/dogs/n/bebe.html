There are 113 licensed dogs named Bebe in NYC.

Bebe is the 495th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 113 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,943. 81% female, 19% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
