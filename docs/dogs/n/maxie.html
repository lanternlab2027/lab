There are 82 licensed dogs named Maxie in NYC.

Maxie is the 677th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 82 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,055. 66% female, 34% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
