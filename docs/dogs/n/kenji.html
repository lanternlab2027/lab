There are 50 licensed dogs named Kenji in NYC.

Kenji is the 1065th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 50 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,651. 98% are male. The most common breed is Shiba Inu. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
