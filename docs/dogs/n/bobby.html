Bobby

Fading

There are 226 licensed dogs named Bobby in NYC.

Bobby is the 221st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 226 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,471. 97% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
