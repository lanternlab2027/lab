Mini

Fading

There are 223 licensed dogs named Mini in NYC.

Mini is the 226th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 223 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,491. 60% female, 40% male. The most common breed is Miniature Australian Shepherd. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
