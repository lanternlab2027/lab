There are 71 licensed dogs named Munchkin in NYC.

Munchkin is the 776th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 71 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,684. 45% female, 55% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
