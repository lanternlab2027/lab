Arrow

There are 54 licensed dogs named Arrow in NYC.

Arrow is the 977th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 54 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,158. 33% female, 67% male. The most common breed is Siberian Husky. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is rising.
