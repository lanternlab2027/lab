There are 98 licensed dogs named Napoleon in NYC.

Napoleon is the 570th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 98 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,393. 100% are male. The most common breed is French Bulldog. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
