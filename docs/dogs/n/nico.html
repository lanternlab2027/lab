Nico

Rising

There are 258 licensed dogs named Nico in NYC.

Nico is the 185th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 258 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,289. 15% female, 85% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
