Ben

There are 132 licensed dogs named Ben in NYC.

Ben is the 421st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 132 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,519. 99% are male. The most common breed is Beagle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
