Sam

There are 388 licensed dogs named Sam in NYC.

Sam is the 124th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 388 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 857. 11% female, 89% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
