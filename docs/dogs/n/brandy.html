There are 180 licensed dogs named Brandy in NYC.

Brandy is the 294th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 180 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,847. 88% female, 12% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
