Cookie

Steady

There are 1,031 licensed dogs named Cookie in NYC.

Cookie is the 25th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,031 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 323. 77% female, 23% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
