There are 127 licensed dogs named Obi in NYC.

Obi is the 435th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 127 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,618. 98% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
