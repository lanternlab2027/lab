Sky

Rising

There are 361 licensed dogs named Sky in NYC.

Sky is the 135th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 361 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 921. 81% female, 19% male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
