Champ

Fading

There are 195 licensed dogs named Champ in NYC.

Champ is the 265th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 195 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,705. 97% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
