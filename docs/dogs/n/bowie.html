Bowie

Rising

There are 368 licensed dogs named Bowie in NYC.

Bowie is the 132nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 368 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 904. 24% female, 76% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
