June

Rising

There are 164 licensed dogs named June in NYC.

June is the 327th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 164 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,028. 93% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
