Rocco

Steady

There are 504 licensed dogs named Rocco in NYC.

Rocco is the 83rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 504 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 660. 99% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
