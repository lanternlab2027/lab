There are 79 licensed dogs named Achilles in NYC.

Achilles is the 701st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 79 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,209. 97% are male. The most common breed is Boxer. Most live in the Bronx. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is fading.
