Stanley

Steady

There are 209 licensed dogs named Stanley in NYC.

Stanley is the 244th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 209 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,591. Over 99% are male. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is steady.
