There are 54 licensed dogs named Bane in NYC.

Bane is the 977th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 54 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,158. 96% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Staten Island. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
