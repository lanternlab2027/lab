Sandy

Fading

There are 437 licensed dogs named Sandy in NYC.

Sandy is the 104th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 437 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 761. 89% female, 11% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
