Holly

Steady

There are 267 licensed dogs named Holly in NYC.

Holly is the 176th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 267 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,245. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
