There are 465 licensed dogs named Maya in NYC.

Maya is the 95th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 465 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 715. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
