There are 76 licensed dogs named Pookie in NYC.

Pookie is the 725th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 76 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,376. 43% female, 57% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
