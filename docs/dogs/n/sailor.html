There are 47 licensed dogs named Sailor in NYC.

Sailor is the 1117th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 47 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,075. 51% female, 49% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
