There are 327 licensed dogs named Mimi in NYC.

Mimi is the 149th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 327 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,017. 98% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
