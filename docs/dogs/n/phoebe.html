Phoebe

Rising

There are 422 licensed dogs named Phoebe in NYC.

Phoebe is the 113th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 422 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 788. 99% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
