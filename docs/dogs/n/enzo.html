There are 286 licensed dogs named Enzo in NYC.

Enzo is the 164th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 286 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,163. 99% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
