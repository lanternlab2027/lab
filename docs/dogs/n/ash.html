There are 100 licensed dogs named Ash in NYC.

Ash is the 561st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 100 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,325. 16% female, 84% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
