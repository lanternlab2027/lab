Roo

Rising

There are 142 licensed dogs named Roo in NYC.

Roo is the 390th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 142 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,342. 66% female, 34% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
