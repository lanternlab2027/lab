There are 203 licensed dogs named Boomer in NYC.

Boomer is the 256th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 203 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,638. 95% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
