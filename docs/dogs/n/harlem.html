There are 78 licensed dogs named Harlem in NYC.

Harlem is the 711th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 78 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,263. 26% female, 74% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
