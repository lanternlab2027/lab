Kenny

Rising

There are 56 licensed dogs named Kenny in NYC.

Kenny is the 945th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 56 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 5,938. 95% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
