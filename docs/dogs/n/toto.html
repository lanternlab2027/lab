There are 106 licensed dogs named Toto in NYC.

Toto is the 532nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 106 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,137. 10% female, 90% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
