Jake

Fading

There are 510 licensed dogs named Jake in NYC.

Jake is the 81st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 510 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 652. Over 99% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
