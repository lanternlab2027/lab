Benny

Steady

There are 433 licensed dogs named Benny in NYC.

Benny is the 107th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 433 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 768. 97% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
