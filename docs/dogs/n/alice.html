Alice

Steady

There are 112 licensed dogs named Alice in NYC.

Alice is the 500th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 112 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,969. 97% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is steady.
