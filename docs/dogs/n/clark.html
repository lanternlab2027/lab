There are 53 licensed dogs named Clark in NYC.

Clark is the 1003rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 53 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 6,274. 98% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
