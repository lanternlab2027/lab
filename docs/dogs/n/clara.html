Clara

There are 43 licensed dogs named Clara in NYC.

Clara is the 1209th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 43 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,733. 98% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
