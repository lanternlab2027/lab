There are 101 licensed dogs named Autumn in NYC.

Autumn is the 557th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 101 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,292. 91% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
