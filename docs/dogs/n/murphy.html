Murphy

There are 475 licensed dogs named Murphy in NYC.

Murphy is the 92nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 475 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 700. 17% female, 83% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
