Walter

Steady

There are 265 licensed dogs named Walter in NYC.

Walter is the 179th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 265 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,255. 99% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
