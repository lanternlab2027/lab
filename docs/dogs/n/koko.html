Koko

There are 177 licensed dogs named Koko in NYC.

Koko is the 296th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 177 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,879. 64% female, 36% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
