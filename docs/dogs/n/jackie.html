Jackie

Fading

There are 149 licensed dogs named Jackie in NYC.

Jackie is the 364th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 149 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,232. 84% female, 16% male. The most common breed is Jack Russell Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
