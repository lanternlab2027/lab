There are 143 licensed dogs named Patches in NYC.

Patches is the 384th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 143 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,325. 43% female, 57% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
