Luna

Rising

There are 3,039 licensed dogs named Luna in NYC.

Luna is the 2nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 3,039 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 109. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
