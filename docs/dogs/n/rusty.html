Rusty

Fading

There are 395 licensed dogs named Rusty in NYC.

Rusty is the 120th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 395 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 842. 96% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
