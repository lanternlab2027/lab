There are 137 licensed dogs named Mika in NYC.

Mika is the 408th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 137 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,427. 91% are female. The most common breed is Shiba Inu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is steady.
