Mickey

Fading

There are 411 licensed dogs named Mickey in NYC.

Mickey is the 116th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 411 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 809. 94% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
