There are 395 licensed dogs named Bandit in NYC.

Bandit is the 120th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 395 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 842. 11% female, 89% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
