There are 490 licensed dogs named Apollo in NYC.

Apollo is the 87th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 490 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 679. 98% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
