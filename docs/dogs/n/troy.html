There are 78 licensed dogs named Troy in NYC.

Troy is the 711th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 78 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,263. 96% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
