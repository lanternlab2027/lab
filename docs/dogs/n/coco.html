Coco

Steady

There are 2,566 licensed dogs named Coco in NYC.

Coco is the 5th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 2,566 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 130. 70% female, 30% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is steady.
