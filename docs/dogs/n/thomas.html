Thomas

Fading

There are 76 licensed dogs named Thomas in NYC.

Thomas is the 725th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 76 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,376. 93% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
