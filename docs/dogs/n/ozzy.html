There are 243 licensed dogs named Ozzy in NYC.

Ozzy is the 203rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 243 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,368. 92% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
