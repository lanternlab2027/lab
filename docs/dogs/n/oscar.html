Oscar

Fading

There are 668 licensed dogs named Oscar in NYC.

Oscar is the 56th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 668 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 498. 99% are male. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
