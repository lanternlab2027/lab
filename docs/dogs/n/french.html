French

Fading

There are 121 licensed dogs named French in NYC.

French is the 463rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 121 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,748. 35% female, 65% male. The most common breed is French Bulldog. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
