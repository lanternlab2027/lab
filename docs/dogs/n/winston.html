There are 696 licensed dogs named Winston in NYC.

Winston is the 54th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 696 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 478. 99% are male. The most common breed is French Bulldog. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
