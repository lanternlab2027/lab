Boo

There are 193 licensed dogs named Boo in NYC.

Boo is the 270th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 193 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,723. 35% female, 65% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
