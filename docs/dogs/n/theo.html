Theo

Rising

There are 358 licensed dogs named Theo in NYC.

Theo is the 136th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 358 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 929. 98% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
