There are 77 licensed dogs named Willy in NYC.

Willy is the 719th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 77 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,319. 97% are male. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
