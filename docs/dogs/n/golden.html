Golden

Rising

There are 67 licensed dogs named Golden in NYC.

Golden is the 816th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 67 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,963. 29% female, 71% male. The most common breed is Golden Retriever. Most live in the Bronx. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
