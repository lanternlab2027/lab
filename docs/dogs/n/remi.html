Remi

There are 168 licensed dogs named Remi in NYC.

Remi is the 316th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 168 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,979. 59% female, 41% male. The most common breed is Goldendoodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
