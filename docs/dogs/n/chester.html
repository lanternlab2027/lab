Chester

Fading

There are 328 licensed dogs named Chester in NYC.

Chester is the 146th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 328 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,014. 98% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
