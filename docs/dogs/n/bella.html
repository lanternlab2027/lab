Bella

Steady

There are 3,457 licensed dogs named Bella in NYC.

Bella is the 1st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 3,457 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 96. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is steady.
