There are 126 licensed dogs named Yoda in NYC.

Yoda is the 443rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 126 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,639. 90% are male. The most common breed is French Bulldog. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
