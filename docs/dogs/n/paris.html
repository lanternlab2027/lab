There are 212 licensed dogs named Paris in NYC.

Paris is the 240th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 212 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,569. 88% female, 12% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
