Rocky

Fading

There are 2,072 licensed dogs named Rocky in NYC.

Rocky is the 7th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 2,072 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 160. 98% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
