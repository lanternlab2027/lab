There are 194 licensed dogs named Chewie in NYC.

Chewie is the 266th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 194 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,714. 12% female, 88% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is fading.
