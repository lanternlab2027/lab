Sugar

Fading

There are 294 licensed dogs named Sugar in NYC.

Sugar is the 158th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 294 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,131. 88% female, 12% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
