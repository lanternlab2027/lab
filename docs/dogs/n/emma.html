Emma

Fading

There are 402 licensed dogs named Emma in NYC.

Emma is the 117th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 402 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 827. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
