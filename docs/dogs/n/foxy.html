Foxy

Fading

There are 211 licensed dogs named Foxy in NYC.

Foxy is the 241st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 211 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,576. 93% are female. The most common breed is Pomeranian. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
