Stevie

Rising

There are 222 licensed dogs named Stevie in NYC.

Stevie is the 228th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 222 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,498. 77% female, 23% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
