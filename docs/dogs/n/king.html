King

Steady

There are 770 licensed dogs named King in NYC.

King is the 45th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 770 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 432. 99% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
