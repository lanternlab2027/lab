There are 328 licensed dogs named Missy in NYC.

Missy is the 146th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 328 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,014. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
