There are 95 licensed dogs named Blackie in NYC.

Blackie is the 586th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 95 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,500. 18% female, 82% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in the Bronx. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
