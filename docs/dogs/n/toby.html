Toby

Steady

There are 1,331 licensed dogs named Toby in NYC.

Toby is the 17th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,331 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 250. 98% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
