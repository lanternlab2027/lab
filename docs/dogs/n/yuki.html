There are 155 licensed dogs named Yuki in NYC.

Yuki is the 344th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 155 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,145. 68% female, 32% male. The most common breed is Shiba Inu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
