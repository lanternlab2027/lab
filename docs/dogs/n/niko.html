There are 214 licensed dogs named Niko in NYC.

Niko is the 236th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 214 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,554. 97% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
