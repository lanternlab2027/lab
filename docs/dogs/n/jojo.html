Jojo

Steady

There are 244 licensed dogs named Jojo in NYC.

Jojo is the 202nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 244 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,363. 31% female, 69% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is steady.
