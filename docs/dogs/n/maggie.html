Maggie

Fading

There are 819 licensed dogs named Maggie in NYC.

Maggie is the 40th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 819 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 406. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
