There are 42 licensed dogs named Sierra in NYC.

Sierra is the 1223rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 42 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,918. 95% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
