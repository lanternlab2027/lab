Biggie

There are 193 licensed dogs named Biggie in NYC.

Biggie is the 270th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 193 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,723. 95% are male. The most common breed is English Bulldog. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
