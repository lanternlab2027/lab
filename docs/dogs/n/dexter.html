Dexter

Fading

There are 485 licensed dogs named Dexter in NYC.

Dexter is the 89th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 485 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 686. 99% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
