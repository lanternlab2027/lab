There are 97 licensed dogs named Cricket in NYC.

Cricket is the 576th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 97 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,428. 78% female, 22% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
