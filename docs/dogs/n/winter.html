Winter

Rising

There are 194 licensed dogs named Winter in NYC.

Winter is the 266th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 194 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,714. 69% female, 31% male. The most common breed is Maltese. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
