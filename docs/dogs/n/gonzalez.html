There are 45 licensed dogs named Gonzalez in NYC.

Gonzalez is the 1164th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 45 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,390. 33% female, 67% male. The most common breed is Maltese. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
