Wesley

There are 79 licensed dogs named Wesley in NYC.

Wesley is the 701st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 79 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,209. 97% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
