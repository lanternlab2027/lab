Scout

Steady

There are 520 licensed dogs named Scout in NYC.

Scout is the 79th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 520 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 639. 61% female, 39% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
