Dee

There are 43 licensed dogs named Dee in NYC.

Dee is the 1209th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 43 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,733. 60% female, 40% male. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is fading.
