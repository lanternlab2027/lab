Akira

There are 65 licensed dogs named Akira in NYC.

Akira is the 832nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 65 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 5,116. 89% female, 11% male. The most common breed is Shiba Inu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
