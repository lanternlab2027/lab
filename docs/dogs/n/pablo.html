There are 165 licensed dogs named Pablo in NYC.

Pablo is the 324th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 165 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,015. 100% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
