Sadie

Steady

There are 753 licensed dogs named Sadie in NYC.

Sadie is the 46th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 753 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 442. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
