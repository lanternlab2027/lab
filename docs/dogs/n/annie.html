Annie

Steady

There are 274 licensed dogs named Annie in NYC.

Annie is the 172nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 274 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,214. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
