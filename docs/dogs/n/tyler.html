Tyler

There are 86 licensed dogs named Tyler in NYC.

Tyler is the 643rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 86 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,867. 98% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
