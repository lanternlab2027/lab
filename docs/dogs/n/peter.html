Peter

There are 111 licensed dogs named Peter in NYC.

Peter is the 506th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 111 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,996. 96% are male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
