Pearl

Rising

There are 175 licensed dogs named Pearl in NYC.

Pearl is the 300th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 175 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,900. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
