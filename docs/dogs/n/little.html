Little

Steady

There are 242 licensed dogs named Little in NYC.

Little is the 204th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 242 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,374. 47% female, 53% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is steady.
