Sunny

Rising

There are 382 licensed dogs named Sunny in NYC.

Sunny is the 127th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 382 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 871. 45% female, 55% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
