Hope

There are 150 licensed dogs named Hope in NYC.

Hope is the 362nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 150 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,217. 94% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
