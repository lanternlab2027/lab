Maple

Rising

There are 239 licensed dogs named Maple in NYC.

Maple is the 206th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 239 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,391. 91% are female. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
