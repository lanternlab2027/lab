There are 34 licensed dogs named Sparkles in NYC.

Sparkles is the 1450th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 34 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 9,781. 91% are female. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2020 and the trend is steady.
