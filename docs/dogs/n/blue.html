Blue

Rising

There are 983 licensed dogs named Blue in NYC.

Blue is the 31st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 983 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 338. 26% female, 74% male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
