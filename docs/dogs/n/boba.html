There are 86 licensed dogs named Boba in NYC.

Boba is the 643rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 86 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,867. 42% female, 58% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
