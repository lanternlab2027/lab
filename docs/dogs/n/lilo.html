Lilo

Rising

There are 189 licensed dogs named Lilo in NYC.

Lilo is the 280th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 189 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,759. 86% female, 14% male. The most common breed is French Bulldog. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
