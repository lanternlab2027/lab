Gino

Fading

There are 76 licensed dogs named Gino in NYC.

Gino is the 725th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 76 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 4,376. 99% are male. The most common breed is Maltese. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is fading.
