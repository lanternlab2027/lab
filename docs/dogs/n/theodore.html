Theodore

Steady

There are 220 licensed dogs named Theodore in NYC.

Theodore is the 231st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 220 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,512. 99% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
