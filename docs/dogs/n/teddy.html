Teddy

Rising

There are 1,906 licensed dogs named Teddy in NYC.

Teddy is the 9th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,906 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 174. 95% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
