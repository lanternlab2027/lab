Andy

Steady

There are 158 licensed dogs named Andy in NYC.

Andy is the 338th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 158 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,105. 92% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
