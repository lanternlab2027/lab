There are 260 licensed dogs named Dakota in NYC.

Dakota is the 183rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 260 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,279. 80% female, 20% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
