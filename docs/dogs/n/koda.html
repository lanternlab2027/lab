Koda

Rising

There are 277 licensed dogs named Koda in NYC.

Koda is the 171st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 277 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,201. 31% female, 69% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
