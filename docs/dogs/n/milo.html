Milo

Rising

There are 1,991 licensed dogs named Milo in NYC.

Milo is the 8th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 1,991 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 167. 98% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
