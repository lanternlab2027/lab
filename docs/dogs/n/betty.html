There are 249 licensed dogs named Betty in NYC.

Betty is the 192nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 249 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,335. 99% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is steady.
