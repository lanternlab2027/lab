Olive

Rising

There are 613 licensed dogs named Olive in NYC.

Olive is the 62nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 613 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 542. 96% are female. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
