Bleu

Rising

There are 115 licensed dogs named Bleu in NYC.

Bleu is the 491st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 115 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,892. 30% female, 70% male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in the Bronx. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
