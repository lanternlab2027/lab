Shea

There are 166 licensed dogs named Shea in NYC.

Shea is the 323rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 166 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,003. 70% female, 30% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
