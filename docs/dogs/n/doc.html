Doc

There are 65 licensed dogs named Doc in NYC.

Doc is the 832nd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 65 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 5,116. 94% are male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2017 and the trend is steady.
