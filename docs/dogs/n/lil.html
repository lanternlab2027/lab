Lil

Steady

There are 110 licensed dogs named Lil in NYC.

Lil is the 511th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 110 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,023. 47% female, 53% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is steady.
