Dog

There are 230 licensed dogs named Dog in NYC.

Dog is the 218th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 230 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,446. 42% female, 58% male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2019 and the trend is fading.
