Dutch

Fading

There are 96 licensed dogs named Dutch in NYC.

Dutch is the 583rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 96 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,464. 94% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is fading.
