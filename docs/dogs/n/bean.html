Bean

There are 217 licensed dogs named Bean in NYC.

Bean is the 234th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 217 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,532. 45% female, 55% male. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2024 and the trend is rising.
