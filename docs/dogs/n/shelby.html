Shelby

Steady

There are 154 licensed dogs named Shelby in NYC.

Shelby is the 349th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 154 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,159. 89% female, 11% male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is steady.
