Remy

There are 428 licensed dogs named Remy in NYC.

Remy is the 109th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 428 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 777. 39% female, 61% male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
