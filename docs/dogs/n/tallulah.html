There are 86 licensed dogs named Tallulah in NYC.

Tallulah is the 643rd most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 86 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,867. 98% are female. The most common breed is Dachshund. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
