There are 246 licensed dogs named Lucas in NYC.

Lucas is the 195th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 246 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,352. 99% are male. The most common breed is Yorkshire Terrier. Most live in Queens. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
