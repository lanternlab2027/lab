Frida

There are 182 licensed dogs named Frida in NYC.

Frida is the 291st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 182 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,827. 99% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2021 and the trend is rising.
