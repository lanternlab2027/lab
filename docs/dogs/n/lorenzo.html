There are 46 licensed dogs named Lorenzo in NYC.

Lorenzo is the 1145th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 46 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 7,229. 96% are male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
