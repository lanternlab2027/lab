There are 183 licensed dogs named Panda in NYC.

Panda is the 288th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 183 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,817. 47% female, 53% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
