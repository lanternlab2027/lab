Star

Steady

There are 232 licensed dogs named Star in NYC.

Star is the 212th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 232 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,433. 90% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
