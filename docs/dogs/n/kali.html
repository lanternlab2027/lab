There are 131 licensed dogs named Kali in NYC.

Kali is the 424th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 131 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 2,538. 95% are female. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is rising.
