There are 455 licensed dogs named Gigi in NYC.

Gigi is the 100th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 455 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 731. 97% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is steady.
