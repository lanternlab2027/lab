There are 107 licensed dogs named Jesse in NYC.

Jesse is the 524th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 107 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 3,108. 28% female, 72% male. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2016 and the trend is fading.
