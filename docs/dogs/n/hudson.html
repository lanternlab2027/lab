Hudson

There are 479 licensed dogs named Hudson in NYC.

Hudson is the 91st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 479 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 694. 92% are male. The most common breed is Labrador Retriever. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2022 and the trend is steady.
