There are 202 licensed dogs named Yoshi in NYC.

Yoshi is the 259th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 202 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,646. 15% female, 85% male. The most common breed is Shiba Inu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is rising.
