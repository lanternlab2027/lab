There are 246 licensed dogs named Cash in NYC.

Cash is the 195th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 246 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,352. 93% are male. The most common breed is Pit Bull. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2023 and the trend is steady.
