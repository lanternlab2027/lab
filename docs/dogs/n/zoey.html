Zoey

Steady

There are 742 licensed dogs named Zoey in NYC.

Zoey is the 47th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 742 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 448. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Shih Tzu. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2018 and the trend is steady.
