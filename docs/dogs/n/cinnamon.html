Cinnamon

Rising

There are 226 licensed dogs named Cinnamon in NYC.

Cinnamon is the 221st most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 226 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,471. 78% female, 22% male. The most common breed is Poodle. Most live in Brooklyn. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2025 and the trend is rising.
