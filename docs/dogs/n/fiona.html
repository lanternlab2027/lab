Fiona

Steady

There are 233 licensed dogs named Fiona in NYC.

Fiona is the 211th most common name among NYC’s licensed dogs: 233 dogs licensed since 2014, about 1 in 1,427. Over 99% are female. The most common breed is Chihuahua. Most live in Manhattan. Its share of new licenses peaked in 2015 and the trend is steady.
