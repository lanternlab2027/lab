Dear friend,

Hello from Lantern Hollow! I'm Juniper Frostwick, but my friends call me Juni. I'm a snowy owl and the newest carrier on the Night Mail. You are my very first pen pal, so this is the most important letter I've ever carried!

I need your help. Last week, a big windstorm came whooshing through and blew away the Great Lantern. That's the glowing lantern on Lighthouse Hill that lights our mail route every night. Without it, we have to fly very slowly and squint a LOT. Postmaster Puffin says my pen pal might be just the one to help. That's you!

I found two clues:

  1. Shiny blue sparkles leading to the Crystal Caves.
  2. Sticky crumbs leading to the Honeycake Library.

I can only fly to one place first. Which should I search?

Can you also draw what you think the Great Lantern looks like? I'll show it to everyone at the Post Office so they know what to look for.

And tell me about you, friend! What's your favorite thing to do outside? Do you have a pet, or a brother or sister?

I'll be waiting by the mailbox. I'm VERY good at waiting, as long as there are snacks.

Your friend from the far, far north,
Juni

P.S. Owls can turn their heads almost all the way around. Can you? Try it!
