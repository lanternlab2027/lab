What the real exam is like

Format and pass mark
The final exam has 50 multiple-choice questions and lasts one hour. You need 70% or more correct to qualify for the Food Protection Certificate.
Source: DOHMH Food Protection Course: course details
The free course
The online course is free and self-paced. It has 15 lessons, and you must pass a quiz to move on from each one. The course is offered in English, Spanish and Chinese, and the study guide and exam in English and 36 other languages. You are prompted to book the exam when you finish the course.
Source: DOHMH: Food Protection Online Course
Fee and payment
The base exam price is $24, plus a $0.60 fee for card payment at the Health Academy. The online exam must be paid by credit card; DOHMH's FAQ says debit cards are not accepted. The College of Staten Island sets its own fees.
Sources: DOHMH: Food Protection Online Course; Online course FAQ, PDF pp. 1–2
Where
The exam is in person at one of two places. The first is the NYC Health Academy, Riverside Health Center, 160 West 100th Street, Manhattan. DOHMH's web page says the third floor and its FAQ says the second, so check your confirmation. The second is the College of Staten Island, 2800 Victory Boulevard.
Sources: DOHMH: Food Protection Online Course; Online course FAQ, PDF p. 2
What to bring
Photo identification and a printout of your confirmation page.
Source: Online course FAQ, PDF p. 2
Classroom option
The in-person course costs $114. It runs 15 hours over five days, and the final exam is on the last day. If you pass, you get your certificate that day. Card payments carry a 2.49% convenience fee.
Source: DOHMH: Food Protection: Classroom
Retakes and the certificate
There is no limit on retakes, but each time you must repeat the entire course and pay for the exam again. After the online-course exam, the certificate arrives within 2 to 6 weeks. It never expires.
Source: Online course FAQ, PDF pp. 1–2
