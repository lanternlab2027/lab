How to export your Goodreads library

Get every book, rating, shelf and read date out of Goodreads as one .csv file, from the Goodreads website.

Last checked: Nov 2026 against the official help pages listed under Sources.

Quick answer

On the Goodreads website: My Books → Import and export (left sidebar, under Tools) → Export Library → download goodreads_library_export.csv.

Export your Goodreads library

  1. Sign in at goodreads.com in a web browser.Use a computer, or on a phone open your browser's menu and choose Request desktop site (Safari: aA → Request Desktop Website). The Goodreads app has no export button.
  2. Click My Books.It's in the top menu.
  3. Click Import and export.It's under Tools in the left sidebar. You can also go straight to goodreads.com/review/import.
  4. Click Export Library.Goodreads starts preparing the file. Big libraries can take a while.
  5. Download the file.When it's ready, a link to the export appears on the same page (refresh if it doesn't). The file is goodreads_library_export.csv.

Troubleshooting

I can't find the export in the Goodreads app

It isn't there. Open goodreads.com in your phone's browser, switch to the desktop site, and follow the steps above.

I clicked Export Library and nothing appeared

Large libraries take longer to prepare. Wait a few minutes and refresh the Import and Export page; the download link appears there when it's ready.

The ISBNs or dates look wrong in Excel

Spreadsheet apps can reformat ISBNs and dates when they open a CSV. Keep the original download untouched, and upload that file to other tools rather than a re-saved copy.

Re-reads only show one date

The Date Read column only holds one date per book. The Read Count column tells you how many times you've read it.

Privacy: what's in a Goodreads export file

The CSV is one row per book on your shelves, with columns like:

  • title, author, ISBN, publisher, binding and page count;
  • your star rating and the Goodreads average rating;
  • your shelves, the date added and date read, read count and owned copies;
  • your reviews and your private notes.

Private notes are hidden on Goodreads but are in this file, so check before you send it to anyone.
