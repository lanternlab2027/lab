How to export a WhatsApp chat

Step-by-step for iPhone and Android, what to do on WhatsApp Web or Desktop, and what the export file actually contains.

Last checked: Nov 2026 against the official help pages listed under Sources.

Quick answer

iPhone: open the chat → tap the contact or group name → Export chat → Without media → save or share.
Android: open the chat → ⋮ More options → More → Export chat → Without media → save or share.
WhatsApp Web / Desktop: no official export steps; export from your phone (see below).

Export a WhatsApp chat on iPhone

  1. Open the chat.Go into the one-to-one or group chat you want to export.
  2. Tap the contact or group name.It's at the top of the chat screen and opens the chat info page.
  3. Tap Export chat.Scroll down the chat info page to find it, near the bottom.
  4. Choose Without media or the option that includes media.Without media is smaller and faster. Including media adds recent photos and videos as separate files (see the limits below).
  5. Pick where the file goes.WhatsApp opens the share sheet. WhatsApp's own examples are Message, Mail and Add to Notes; Save to Files keeps it on your phone. On iPhone the export arrives as a .zip with a _chat.txt inside.

About our check: WhatsApp's article has Android and iOS tabs, but the text our checker could read was the Android version. The iPhone route above (chat name → Export chat) is how the iPhone app is laid out; the media option may be labelled Attach Media or Include media depending on version. If a label differs, follow WhatsApp's article.

Export a WhatsApp chat on Android

  1. Open the chat.Go into the one-to-one or group chat you want to export.
  2. Tap More options ⋮, then More, then Export chat.The three-dot menu is in the top-right corner. These are the exact menu names in WhatsApp's help article.
  3. Tap Without media or Include media.Without media is smaller and faster.
  4. Choose how to send or save it.Pick an app from the list, for example email, Google Drive or a Files app. The chat is a .txt file named like WhatsApp Chat with …, sometimes inside a .zip.

WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop

WhatsApp's "How to export your chat history" article only gives instructions for the Android and iOS apps. We found no official instructions for exporting a chat from WhatsApp Web or the Windows or Mac desktop apps, so we won't guess at a menu that may not exist.

If you need the file on a computer, export it on your phone using the steps above, then send it to yourself: email it, save it to iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or AirDrop it.

Limits: what an export does and doesn't include

  • Media is partial. WhatsApp says that if you include media, the most recent media is attached, not every photo and video ever sent.
  • It's not a backup. WhatsApp says the export is a text file and can't be re-imported into WhatsApp. To move or restore chats, use WhatsApp's chat backup instead.
  • Message count limits. The current official article gives no number. Older versions of WhatsApp's FAQ, still widely quoted, said about 10,000 recent messages with media and 40,000 without media, blamed on maximum email sizes. We couldn't confirm those numbers on WhatsApp's current page, so treat them as unconfirmed. If a long chat seems cut short, export Without media.
  • One phone's history. The export can only contain messages that are on the phone you export from.

Troubleshooting

"Export chat" is missing or greyed out in one chat

Someone in the chat may have turned on Advanced Chat Privacy. WhatsApp introduced it in April 2025, and when it's on, participants can't export the chat. Tap the chat name, then Advanced Chat Privacy, to see whether it's on. Only ask the person who turned it on; it exists so people can keep a chat from being exported.

"Export chat" is missing in every chat

Update WhatsApp from the App Store or Google Play first. Some users report that the option isn't offered in certain countries (press reports in 2019 said it was removed in Germany); WhatsApp's help center doesn't document this, so we can't confirm which countries are affected.

The export is huge or won't send

Export again and choose Without media. Email apps often reject large attachments; saving to Files or Drive avoids that.

Can I put the exported chat back into WhatsApp?

No. WhatsApp says chat history exported this way can't be re-imported, because it's a text file and not a backup file.

Privacy: what's in a WhatsApp export file

The export is a plain-text file. Anyone who has the file can read it; WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption doesn't cover a copy that has left the app. It contains:

  • every message's date and time and the sender, shown as the name saved in your contacts, or as a phone number for people you haven't saved;
  • the full text of every message, including links, from everyone in the chat, not just you;
  • system lines such as people joining or leaving, and placeholders like <Media omitted> when you export without media;
  • if you include media, recent photos, videos, voice notes and documents as separate files.

Other people's messages and phone numbers are in there, so think before you share the file, and delete copies from email or cloud storage once you're done.
