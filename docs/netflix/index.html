How to download your viewing history

Netflix gives you a plain .csv file with a Title and a Date for everything a profile watched. It downloads instantly. Upload it here as it is.

Viewing activity (instant, one profile)
  1. In a web browser (not the TV or phone app), sign in at netflix.com and go to your Account page.
  2. Select Profiles, then choose the profile you want.
  3. Open Viewing activity.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and select Download all. You get NetflixViewingHistory.csv.
  5. Upload that file here. On a phone, use your browser (Safari or Chrome); the file lands in Downloads or Files.

Steps from Netflix’s help center: How to see viewing history and device activity.

Full data request (every profile, takes longer)

The account owner can also request a copy of all account data at netflix.com/account/getmyinfo. Netflix says it can take up to 30 days (Netflix help). The zip it sends has CONTENT_INTERACTION/ViewingActivity.csv, which covers every profile. Upload the zip or that file; you can switch between profiles above the cards, and trailers and previews are left out.

What can the cards know?

The Download all file only has a title and a date, with no times or durations. So the cards count titles and days, not minutes; the hours figure is an estimate (45 minutes an episode, 1 hour 50 for a movie). A series is spotted from titles like Show: Season 1: Episode; anything without a season or a repeated show name counts as a movie or special. Netflix may list a title you watched twice only once, at the latest date.

Your history never leaves your device. How we know:

There’s no server to send it to

This page is plain files on GitHub Pages. Your file is read, counted and drawn into cards by JavaScript running in this browser tab. Close the tab and it’s gone.

The browser blocks it anyway

The page ships a Content-Security-Policy that only lets it talk to one outside address: the optional email signup below, which sends just your email. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.

It’s open source

Every line is on GitHub: lanternlab2027/lab → docs/netflix. The zip helper (JSZip) is copied into the folder, not loaded from a CDN.

Try it offline

Load this page, turn on airplane mode, then upload your file. It still works, because nothing needs the internet.

Get next year’s Streaming Wrapped

Optional. We’ll email you once when the next edition is ready. We only send your email address; nothing from your viewing history is ever included.

Questions

Does Netflix have its own Wrapped?

Not a personal year in review like Spotify’s. That’s why this exists: it builds one from the viewing history Netflix already lets you download.

Is this made by Netflix?

No. Streaming Wrapped is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Netflix, and it uses no Netflix logos or artwork.

How is the viewer personality picked?

From your numbers alone: your biggest day, how much of your year went to one show, your movie share, weekend share, streaks, the show you kept coming back to, and how bunched-up your watching is. The strongest signal wins.

It says “doesn’t look like a Netflix viewing history”

Upload the original .csv from Viewing activity → Download all. Files re-saved from Excel or Numbers, other languages and other date formats (11/14/26, 14/11/2026, 14.11.26) all work, but the file needs a title column and a date column.

Shared profile? Kids’ shows everywhere?

Each profile downloads separately, so pick your own profile before you tap Download all.

Is it free?

Yes, completely.
