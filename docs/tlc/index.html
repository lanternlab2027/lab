What the real exam is like

Questions, pass mark and time
The exam has 80 multiple-choice questions. You need a score of 70% or higher, which means 56 correct answers. You have up to 2 hours, there are no breaks, and you take it on a computer at a PSI test center.
Sources: TLC Licensing Education; TLC/PSI Candidate Bulletin, PDF p. 5
What it covers
The exam tests what is taught in the New TLC Driver License Course (the 3-day, 24-hour course) and the Passenger Assistance and Wheelchair Accessible Vehicle (WAV) training. TLC's study guide lists ten areas: government, licensing, vehicle requirements, passenger solicitation, customer service and service refusal, accessible dispatch rules, proper conduct, Vision Zero and safety, penalties, and New York City geography. The rules behind them are TLC Rules Chapter 80 (drivers) and Chapter 53 (accessible vehicles).
Sources: TLC Licensing Education; TLC Study Guide, PDF p. 5
Before you can book it
Apply online through LARS first. Then finish the 3-day course at a TLC-authorized school. PSI emails you a registration link after the school reports that you completed it. TLC says to finish the WAV course before the exam too. Everything has to be done within 90 days of your application, including the exam, a drug test, fingerprints, a medical form, a 6-hour defensive driving course and a 10-minute sex trafficking awareness video.
Sources: TLC Licensing Education; TLC: Get a TLC Drivers License
Fees and retakes
Each exam attempt costs $49, paid to PSI. You can retake it as many times as you need until your application expires, but you must wait 24 hours after a fail. Cancel or reschedule at least 2 days ahead, or you lose the $49. Other costs listed by TLC: $252 for the three-year license, $245–$250 for the 3-day course, $50–$125 for WAV training, $34 for the drug test, $90.25 for fingerprints and $25–$75 for defensive driving. All TLC fees are non-refundable.
Sources: TLC: Get a TLC Drivers License; TLC/PSI Candidate Bulletin, PDF pp. 5–6
Where you take it
PSI and partner test centers in all five boroughs: 34 Marconi Street and 3250 Westchester Ave (Bronx), 527 Coney Island Avenue (Brooklyn), 566 7th Ave (Manhattan), 102-50 67th Drive, Forest Hills (Queens), and 1911 Richmond Avenue (Staten Island).
Source: TLC Licensing Education
On the day
Arrive 15 minutes early with your state-issued driver license, and fill out a TLC Sign-in Registration Form. PSI gives you a Geographia five-borough street atlas, a Manhattan Address Key, blank paper and a pencil. Calculators and phones are not allowed. Your pass or fail result appears on screen as soon as you submit.
Source: TLC/PSI Candidate Bulletin, PDF pp. 6–7
Languages and English
You can take the exam in English, Arabic, Bengali, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Spanish or Urdu. The current TLC licensing rules (Chapter 80, §80-04) and TLC's application page list no separate English proficiency test.
Sources: TLC/PSI Candidate Bulletin, PDF p. 3; TLC Rules Chapter 80, §80-04

About the mock exam: it matches the published format of 80 questions in 2 hours. The real exam also asks map and photo questions about NYC geography, such as naming a crossing or picking the most direct route, and you answer those with the atlas PSI provides. This bank covers geography in words only. TLC does not publish how many questions come from each topic.

Heads-up on fares: TLC's study guide is dated May 2019, and its fare figures are out of date (for example, it lists a $2.50 initial charge and a $52 JFK flat fare). This bank uses TLC's current Taxi Fare page instead: $3.00 initial charge, $70 JFK flat fare, $20 Newark surcharge, plus the congestion surcharge and the 75-cent congestion pricing toll.

TLC rules, fees and fares change. Always check the latest TLC licensing page before you apply.
