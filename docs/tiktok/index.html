Scroll Wrapped

A free TikTok Wrapped 2026, made from your own TikTok data export: videos watched, estimated minutes, your busiest day, the 3am scroll clock, your longest doomscroll, streaks, likes and top searches, as cards sized for stories. Wrapped for your TikTok data, no sign-up.

Your data never leaves your device. Messages are skipped.

Step 1: Request your export now (it takes 1–4 days)

TikTok has to prepare the file, so ask for it today. You’ll get a notification when it’s ready, then you have 4 days to download it.

  1. In the TikTok app, tap Profile, then the ☰ menu (top right).
  2. Tap Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data.
  3. On the Request data tab, choose All data (or make sure Activity / watch history is ticked).
  4. Pick the file format JSON. (TXT works here too.)
  5. Tap Request data. That’s it for now.
  6. When TikTok notifies you, go back to Download your data → the Download data tab → Download. Do it within 4 days, or the file expires and you’ll need to ask again.
  7. Come back here and drop in the zip exactly as it arrived.

On a computer: on tiktok.com, open Settings → Privacy and find Download your data. Menu names vary a little between versions.

Based on TikTok’s help page Requesting your data (file “available for up to 4 days”; it “may take a few days” to prepare). Big, older accounts can wait longer.

What the cards can know

Which files are read

From the JSON export (user_data_tiktok.json, or user_data.json in older exports) or the TXT export (Your Activity/Watch History.txt and friends), we read:

  • Watch history: the date and time of each video (the link is only used to drop exact duplicates).
  • Likes, comments, shares and logins: counted by date only. Comment text, IP addresses and devices are never kept.
  • Searches: date and search term. Terms only appear on cards if you switch them on.
How the minutes are estimated

The export lists each video you opened with a timestamp, but not how long you watched it. So the minutes figure is an estimate: number of videos × 30 seconds. That’s our assumption for an average video, mixing quick swipes with full watches; the card says so. The doomscroll card is measured, not estimated: it’s the longest run of videos where every gap was under 2 minutes.

Times and time zones

TikTok writes every time in UTC. The cards convert to your device’s time zone; if you were travelling or the times look off, pick another zone above the cards. “After midnight” means midnight to 4:59am in that zone.

Your data never leaves your device. How we know:

Direct messages are skipped entirely

Your export includes your DMs. We don’t use them. In a zip, the message files are never even unzipped. In a JSON file, the messages section is thrown away the moment the file is opened, before anything is counted. No message content is read into the cards, shown or stored.

There’s no server to send it to

This page is plain files on GitHub Pages. Your export is read, counted and drawn into cards by JavaScript running in this browser tab. Close the tab and it’s gone.

The browser blocks it anyway

The page ships a Content-Security-Policy that only lets it talk to one outside address: the optional email reminder below, which sends just your email. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.

It’s open source, and works offline

Every line is on GitHub: lanternlab2027/lab → docs/tiktok. The zip helper (JSZip) is copied into the folder. Load the page, turn on airplane mode, then upload: it still works.

Email me a reminder when Wrapped season opens

Optional. One email in late November, so you remember to request your export in time. We only send your email address; nothing from your export is ever included.

Questions

Is there an official TikTok Wrapped?

TikTok doesn’t give you a Spotify-style recap built from your full watch history. This page builds one from the data export TikTok already lets you download.

Is this made by TikTok?

No. Scroll Wrapped is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance, and it uses no TikTok logos or artwork.

Why does it take days?

TikTok prepares the file on its side. Small accounts can get it within a day; bigger accounts usually wait a few days. Once it’s ready, the download link lasts 4 days. Request it now and sign up for the reminder if you’ll forget.

My numbers look different from the app

The in-app watch history only shows recent videos; the export goes back further. TikTok may also leave out the last day or two of activity. Counts depend on what the export contains.

It says “doesn’t look like a TikTok data export”

Upload the zip from Download your data as it arrived, or the user_data_tiktok.json inside it. For a TXT export, upload the zip or the Watch History.txt file (older exports call it Browsing History.txt). If your account language isn’t English, the TXT file names may be translated; the JSON export works in every language.

Is it free?

Yes, completely.
