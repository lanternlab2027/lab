What the real exam is like

Format
A timed, computer-based multiple-choice test on a touch screen. Each question has four answer choices, and you need at least 70% to pass. There is no penalty for guessing.
Sources: FDNY: Understanding COFs; General Notice of Exam, PDF p. 4; F-03/F-04 booklet, PDF p. 7
Number of questions
FDNY says most exams have 20–25 questions. In the booklets: F-01 has 20 questions in 30 minutes, F-03 has 25 in 38 minutes, and F-60 has 25 in 38 minutes. S-95 is longer, with 35 questions in 53 minutes.
Sources: Understanding COFs; booklets: F-01 p. 5, F-03 p. 5, F-60 pp. 3–4, S-95 p. 5
Fee
Most exams cost $25, and card payments carry a 2% convenience fee. You pay by credit/debit card, check or money order. Cash is no longer accepted.
Sources: Understanding COFs; FDNY: Certificates of Fitness; General Notice of Exam, PDF p. 3
Where and when
The exam is taken at FDNY Headquarters, 9 MetroTech Center, Brooklyn (Flatbush Avenue entrance). Most exams are walk-in, Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM, except legal holidays. You must apply and pay online before you arrive and bring a government-issued photo ID.
Sources: FDNY: Certificates of Fitness; General Notice of Exam, PDF pp. 2, 4
On the day
Exams are in English only. A paper dictionary is the only outside aid allowed; phones and other electronics must be turned off. Results come right after the exam, and if you pass you get your card the same day. Plan on at least 2 hours, and you can take up to 2 exams per day.
Sources: Understanding COFs; General Notice of Exam, PDF pp. 4–5
After you pass
Most Certificates of Fitness must be renewed every three years. The renewal fee for most 3-year certificates is $15.
Source: General Notice of Exam, PDF p. 6

FDNY rules and fees change. Always check the latest booklet and FDNY's Certificates of Fitness page before your exam.
