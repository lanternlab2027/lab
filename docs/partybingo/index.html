How watch-party bingo works

  1. Pick a list. Start from a ready-made list for the big game, awards night, holiday movie night, a reality TV finale, a family holiday dinner or New Year's Eve, or write your own. You need at least 24 squares.
  2. Share one link. Text it to the group, or show the QR code on the TV. Each guest who opens it gets a card shuffled just for them, with a free space in the middle.
  3. Tap as it happens. Five in a row, column or diagonal is a BINGO. The winner gets a picture to send to the chat: "BINGO at 9:42pm with 'Ref misses an obvious call'".
  4. Prefer paper? Print as many unique cards as you need, one per page.

Questions

Does every guest really get a different card?

Yes. When someone opens the link, their phone picks a random number and uses it to shuffle your list, then remembers it so a refresh shows the same card. With the Big Game list of 60+ squares there are more possible cards than people on Earth. Even with exactly 24 squares the positions are different on every card.

Is this for the football game in February?

It works for any football watch party, including the big game on Sunday, February 14, 2027 (yes, it's also Valentine's Day). The Big Game Sunday list covers plays, refs, the broadcast, the commercials and the halftime show. We're not affiliated with any league, network or event.

How do I check a winner's card?

When someone gets a bingo, their share includes a "check my card" link. It opens their exact card and marks, so everyone can see the five in a row.

Where are the squares and marks stored?

The squares are inside the link (the part after the #, which browsers never send to a server). Each guest's card and marks stay in their own browser. There's no account, database, ads or tracking.

Can I print bingo cards?

Yes. After you create the game, choose how many cards (up to 100) and press Print. Each card is unique and numbered, one per letter-size page, in black and white.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No ads and no sign-up. Prizes and bets are up to you; we don't run any.
