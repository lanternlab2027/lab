Lantern Lab NYC

Free tools for NYC building owners and city license exam takers.

For building owners

2027 Owner Compliance CalendarFree printable PDF of NYC deadlines for 1–20 unit buildings, month by month, with official sources. Building WatchLook up open HPD, DOB and ECB violations for your building, plus a 12-month compliance calendar. Energy Grade & LL97Your building's energy letter grade and a rough estimate of Local Law 97 emissions penalties. Hearing WatchUpcoming OATH hearings and past missed hearings for an address, summons or BBL, with calendar reminders. Should I Appeal My Taxes?Compare your DOF market value with recent sales of similar homes, before the March appeal deadlines. House HistoryCited history reports for NYC houses, from old maps, newspapers and city records.

Exam practice

FDNY Certificates of FitnessPractice tests for F-01, F-03, F-60 and S-95, cited to the FDNY study material. NYC Sightseeing GuidePractice questions and a full 150-question mock for the DCWP guide license exam. NYC Home Improvement ContractorPractice questions and a 30-question mock for the DCWP HIC license exam. NYC Process ServerPractice questions and a 35-question mock for the DCWP process server license exam. NYC TLC Driver Exam145 practice questions and a timed 80-question mock for the TLC driver license exam. NYC Food ProtectionPractice questions and a timed 50-question mock for the DOHMH food protection exam.

Apps

Group Chat WrappedTurn a WhatsApp or Telegram group export into shareable year-in-review cards. Runs entirely on your device. Reading WrappedYour year in books from a Goodreads or StoryGraph export: shareable cards, made on your device. Fantasy League AwardsWeekly and season awards cards for your Sleeper fantasy league: bench boss, heartbreak, luckiest win and more. Secret Santa, no sign-upDraw names and send everyone a private link. No emails, no accounts.
Independent tools. Not affiliated with the City of New York or any city agency. Information only, not legal advice; always confirm requirements on nyc.gov.
