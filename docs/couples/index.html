What's in the deck

  • Messages each, and who sends the first text of the morning
  • Typical reply time for each of you (the median, ignoring gaps over 12 hours)
  • How many goodnights (goodnight, gn, buenas noches, bonne nuit, gute Nacht, boa noite, buonanotte…)
  • Your most-used emojis, and the one you both use most
  • Your longest daily streak and your busiest day
  • How much of your texting happens after 11 PM
  • I love yous, in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese or Italian
  • The date of the first message in your file, and a summary card

Fun facts, not a score. Nothing here rates your relationship, and the cards never show what anyone wrote.

How to export your chat

Export the 1:1 chat without media. It's faster, and the cards only need the text.

WhatsApp on iPhone
  1. Open WhatsApp and go into the chat with your partner.
  2. Tap their name at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Export chat.
  4. Choose Without media.
  5. Pick Save to Files, then come back here, tap Choose your chat export and pick the .zip file.

More detail and troubleshooting: How to export a WhatsApp chat.

WhatsApp on Android
  1. Open WhatsApp and go into the chat with your partner.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap More, then Export chat.
  4. Choose Without media.
  5. Save it to your phone (for example to Drive or Files), then upload the .txt or .zip here.

Full guide: How to export a WhatsApp chat.

Telegram (Desktop app)
  1. Telegram's phone apps can't export chats, so use Telegram Desktop on a computer.
  2. Open the chat with your partner, click the ⋮ menu, then Export chat history.
  3. Untick photos, videos and files. Set Format to Machine-readable JSON.
  4. Click Export, then upload the result.json file from the export folder.

Full guide: How to export Telegram chat history.

iMessage or Instagram?

iPhone can't export an iMessage conversation as a file, so iMessage isn't supported. Instagram DMs aren't supported yet either. WhatsApp and Telegram both have a built-in export, which is what this page reads.

Your chat never leaves your device. How we know:

There's no server to send it to

This page is plain files on GitHub Pages. Your export is read, counted and drawn into cards by JavaScript running in this browser tab. Close the tab and it's gone.

The browser blocks it anyway

The page ships a Content-Security-Policy that only lets it talk to one outside address: the optional email signup, which sends just your email. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.

Numbers, not messages

The cards only show counts, dates and the names you choose (initials by default). Words like “goodnight” are counted, but no message is ever shown.

Open source, and works offline

Every line is on GitHub. Load this page, turn on airplane mode, then upload your chat. It still works.

Get a reminder before Valentine's Day

Optional. We only send your email address. Nothing from your chat is ever included.

Questions

Does it say who loves who more?

No, and it never will. Every card is a fun fact about the two of you. The I-love-you card shows one shared total, and nothing is ranked or graded.

Can my partner's messages end up on a card?

No. Cards show counts, dates and emojis only. Names show as initials by default; you can switch to first names or type your own nicknames before sharing.

Can I use it for a group chat?

This one is for chats between two people. For groups, use Group Chat Wrapped.

Is this made by WhatsApp or Telegram?

No. Couples Chat Wrapped is an independent project that works with the export files those apps already let you make. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp, Meta or Telegram.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark on the cards beyond a small “Make yours” line.
