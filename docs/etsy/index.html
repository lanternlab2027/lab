About Form 1099-K

Etsy (as a payment platform) generally has to send a Form 1099-K only when your gross payments for the year are over $20,000 and more than 200 transactions. The One, Big, Beautiful Bill put that federal threshold back in place retroactively, and the IRS repeated it in January 2026. Some states have lower thresholds of their own, so you may still get one.

No 1099-K does not mean no tax. The threshold only decides whether the form is sent; business income is reportable either way. The 1099-K shows gross payments, before Etsy’s fees and refunds, so it will not match the net above.

Sources: IRS, IRS issues FAQs on Form 1099-K threshold under the One, Big, Beautiful Bill; dollar limit reverts to $20,000 (IR-2025-107, Oct. 23, 2025) and IR-2026-03 (Jan. 8, 2026), both checked Nov. 15, 2026. Rules can change; your accountant should confirm what applies to your 2026 return.

How to download your Etsy files

Etsy lets sellers download their shop data any time, as CSV files, on a computer browser. Get the year’s orders for the cards, and the monthly statements for the tax summary.

1. Orders and order items (for the cards)
  1. Sign in at etsy.com on a computer and open Shop Manager.
  2. Go to Settings → Options, then the Download Data tab.
  3. Under Orders, pick the CSV Type “Orders”, choose the Year, and leave Month empty to get the whole year. Select Download CSV. You get EtsySoldOrders2026.csv.
  4. Do it again with CSV Type “Order Items” for EtsySoldOrderItems2026.csv. This one has your listing titles, so it powers the best-seller card.
  5. Drop both files here together.

Etsy’s own instructions: How to Download a Spreadsheet of Your Sold Transactions (Etsy Help, checked Nov. 15, 2026). Menu names change from time to time.

2. Monthly statements (for the tax summary)
  1. In Shop Manager, select Finances → Payment account. (In the Etsy Seller app it’s under More → Finances.)
  2. Open Monthly statements (or “View all monthly statements” at the bottom of the page).
  3. Choose a month and select Download CSV. Repeat for each month of the year; each file is like etsy_statement_2026_3.csv.
  4. Select all twelve at once and drop them here, with or without the order files.

Etsy’s own page: How to Manage Your Payment Account (Etsy Help, checked Nov. 15, 2026). That page describes monthly statements but doesn’t spell out the CSV button; seller guides (e.g. Craftybase) describe Finances → Monthly statements → Download CSV.

Which columns does this read?

Orders: sale date, order ID, number of items, order value, discount, shipping, sales tax, order total, adjusted order total, status, currency, ship state and ship country. The buyer user ID is scrambled into a one-way code on the spot (to count repeat buyers) and the original is thrown away.

Order items: sale date, order ID, item name, listing ID, transaction ID, quantity, price, item total, currency, ship state and country.

Statements: date, type, title, info (only to find an order number), currency, amount, fees & taxes and net.

Never read: buyer full, first and last names, street, city and zip, ship-to name, usernames, payment method, coupon codes and personalization notes (Variations). Files like deposits, payments, reviews and listings are skipped.

Your shop data stays on your device. How we know:

There’s no server to send it to

This page is plain files on GitHub Pages. Your CSVs are read, counted and drawn by JavaScript in this browser tab. Close the tab and they’re gone.

The browser blocks it anyway

The page’s Content-Security-Policy only allows two outside addresses: the optional email signup, which sends just your email, and our page-view counter, which never sees your files. We count anonymous page views with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal data). No ad trackers, no third-party scripts or fonts.

Buyers stay anonymous

Name, address and email columns are skipped when the file is read, so they are never in memory to show. Places are counted by state or country; repeat buyers are a percentage from scrambled IDs.

It’s open source

Every line is on GitHub: lanternlab2027/lab → docs/etsy. The zip writer (JSZip) is copied into the folder, not loaded from a CDN. Try it in airplane mode.

Remind me at tax time

Optional. One email in January to download your statements and run the summary, and one when next year’s recap is ready. We only send your email address; nothing from your shop is ever included.

Questions

Is this made by Etsy?

No. Seller Year in Review is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Etsy, Inc., and uses no Etsy logos or brand colors. It just reads the files Etsy lets you download.

Why don’t the card totals match the tax summary?

The cards use your orders file: item sales after discounts plus shipping charged, minus refunds the file shows. The tax summary uses your statements, which count money when Etsy posts it (a late-December order can land in January) and include every fee. Sales tax that Etsy collects and pays for you is left out of both.

How is “net” worked out?

Gross sales (statement sales minus the sales tax Etsy collected) minus refunds to buyers, minus every Etsy fee (transaction, processing, listing, Offsite Ads, Etsy Ads, shipping labels, subscriptions, regulatory and VAT on fees), plus any other credits. We check it against the sum of the Net column in your statements and say if they disagree. Deposits to your bank are not income and are left out.

My shop sells in more than one currency

Statements are in your payment account’s currency; orders are in your listing currency. Nothing is ever added across currencies: pick the currency next to the year, and the page says what was left out.

Where is the best-seller card?

It needs the Order Items CSV, which has your listing titles. The Orders CSV alone has counts and totals only.

It says it can’t read my file

Upload the CSV exactly as Etsy gave it, not a copy re-saved from a spreadsheet (which can change dates and commas). If Etsy renamed a column, tell us on GitHub (without your file) and we’ll add the new name.

Is it free?
