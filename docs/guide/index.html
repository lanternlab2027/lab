What the real exam is like

Questions and pass mark
The exam has 150 questions, and you need at least 97 correct to pass. There are different versions of the exam. DCWP's exam FAQ calls the questions multiple choice, while the Study Reference says they are either multiple choice or true/false.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 5; Study Reference, PDF p. 1
Time limit
DCWP does not publish a time limit in its checklist, exam FAQ or Study Reference. Ask ExamBuilder when you book. The mock timers on this site use a practice pace of one minute per question.
Fees
The exam fee is $50. It is nonrefundable and separate from the license fee. The license runs for 2 years and expires on March 31 of even years, so the license fee is $13 to $63 depending on when you apply. Card payments carry a nonrefundable 2% convenience fee.
Source: DCWP license checklist
Booking and where
ExamBuilder runs the exam. You can book 24 hours after DCWP processes your application, online at dcaschedule.exambuilder.com or by phone at (646) 974-8244. You then have 60 days to take and pass it. Test centers in multiple locations citywide are open 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., seven days a week. Applicants granted a disability or language accommodation test at the DCWP Licensing Center, 42 Broadway.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 1–2
Attempts
You have two attempts. After a first failure, you must wait 24 hours before booking your second attempt. After a second failure, you must pay the $50 exam fee again to retake it. If you miss an exam without rescheduling by 5:00 p.m. the day before, it counts as a fail.
Sources: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 2–3; Study Reference, PDF p. 2
On the day
Bring one government-issued photo ID with your signature. You get your score and a pass or fail result right after you finish, and ExamBuilder reports the result to DCWP.
Source: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 3
Who takes it
New applicants only; renewals do not need to retake it. Applicants must be at least 18.
Sources: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 1; DCWP license checklist

What this question bank covers

This bank uses only free official nyc.gov pages from DCWP, NYC DOT, the Landmarks Preservation Commission, Cultural Affairs, DCAS, City Planning and the Office of the Mayor.
