    How the no-account pool works

    1. You make a pool link. Pick a name, the scoring and a lock time. All of that is packed into the link itself. Post it in your group chat.
    2. Each player fills in a bracket from that link and taps "Get my picks link". That link holds their name and all 75 picks. They send it back to you.
    3. You paste the picks links into the standings page. It adds up scores from the results we post on this site after each session of games, and makes the awards at the end. Share the standings link back to the group.

    Nothing is stored on a server. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and we never see your pool or anyone's picks. Everything lives in the links, in the part after the #, which browsers don't send to any website. The page only downloads our results file to do the math in your browser. Anyone who has a link can open it, so share links only with your group.

    Tamper check. Each picks link carries a checksum. If someone edits their picks by hand, the standings page flags the entry as "edited link". Each link also remembers when it was made, and anything after the lock shows as "late". Both are honest-player checks (the time comes from the player's own device), not security.     What's new in 2027: the 76-team format

    The NCAA announced in May 2026 that both Division I basketball tournaments grow from 68 to 76 teams starting in 2027. The old four play-in games become a 12-game Opening Round with 24 teams, and the 12 winners join 52 teams that start in the round of 64.

    • Who plays in the Opening Round: the 12 lowest-seeded automatic qualifiers (conference champions) play six games, and the 12 lowest-seeded at-large teams play the other six. The committee seeds all 76 teams and pairs teams that sit next to each other on its overall seed list.
    • Where the 8 extra teams slot in: in the published plan, the at-large games decide two No. 11 seeds and four No. 12 seeds, and the automatic-qualifier games decide two No. 15 seeds and four No. 16 seeds. So every region's 12 and 16 lines start with an Opening Round game, and two regions also get an 11 game while the other two get a 15 game. That's how this bracket is drawn.
    • Not final until Selection Sunday: the committee can adjust pairings (to avoid rematches, for travel, or because the closest teams on the seed list land on a different line), and it sets which regions get the 11 and 15 games and which regions meet in the national semifinals. Once the bracket is revealed on March 14, 2027, we post the field here and the sheet updates.

    Questions

    How do I print the 76-team bracket on one page?

    Tap "Print the blank bracket". It prints only the bracket, landscape, sized to fit both US Letter and A4. In the print dialog choose "Save as PDF" for a PDF. The PDF view shows the same page full-screen.

    How many games are in a 76-team bracket?

    75: 12 Opening Round games, then 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 and 1. Every game knocks out one team, and 76 teams leave one champion.

    Why are the teams "East 1", "East 16a" and so on?

    The field isn't picked until Selection Sunday, March 14, 2027. Until then the bracket shows seed lines. "16a" and "16b" are the two teams in an Opening Round game for a 16 seed. When the field is posted, real names fill in, and pools that use the official field update by themselves.

    Is this the official March Madness bracket?

    No. This is an independent, free bracket and pool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the NCAA. Team names, once posted, are used only to identify the teams.

    What if a player changes their picks after the lock?

    A new picks link records the time it was made, so the standings mark it late. If the link is edited by hand, the checksum no longer matches and it's marked "edited link". Both rely on honesty; it's a pool for bragging rights.

    How big can a pool be?

    Up to 250 entries in one standings link. Past about 50 entries, pasting links gets tedious, so a group chat export pasted in one go works best: the page finds every picks link in the text.

    Where do the scores come from?

    We update a results file on this site after each session of games. Your browser downloads it and scores every bracket on your own device.
