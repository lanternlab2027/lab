What an appeal can and can't change

Two separate agencies, two different requests

  • NYC Tax Commission (the appeal). An independent agency that can lower your assessed value, fix your tax class, or review an exemption. It can't change the market value figure on your notice, and it can't change the building description. Filing is free for homes like these. The $175 fee applies only when the assessed value is $2 million or more.
  • Department of Finance (DOF) Request for Review. This asks DOF itself to take another look at the tentative value. Use DOF's Request to Update to fix wrong facts such as square footage, units or stories. Neither one replaces a Tax Commission application filed on time.

Class 1 (1-3 family homes): why a high market value may not matter

  • Your assessed value starts at 6% of DOF's market value. State law also caps increases at 6% a year and 20% over five years. Work such as additions or renovations, and an exemption that ends, aren't covered by the caps.
  • Because of the caps, most Class 1 homes are taxed on less than 6% of market value. DOF calls the value you're actually taxed on the effective market value: your assessed value divided by 6%.
  • Your tax only goes down if you show that the home is worth less than the effective market value. That's often well below the market value printed on the notice. It's also why your assessed value can keep rising after the market value falls.
  • The Tax Commission's own test (Form TC108): multiply what you think the home would sell for by 6%. If the result isn't below the assessed value on your notice, don't file.

Small buildings in Class 2 and 4

  • Assessed value is 45% of market value. Class 2A, 2B and 2C (4-10 units) increases are capped at 8% a year and 30% over five years. Other Class 2 and Class 4 changes are phased in over five years.
  • DOF generally values rental buildings by their income, so comparing sale prices per square foot is only a rough cross-check. Appeals for rental property need an income and expense statement, except for residential buildings with 6 or fewer units and no commercial space.
