How we handle your data

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Each page ships a Content-Security-Policy, so the browser itself blocks any connection we haven't listed here.

  • Wrapped apps: zero uploadsYour chat, library, viewing history, workouts or dating export is read and drawn in your browser. The file never leaves your device.
  • Fantasy League AwardsReads public league data from Sleeper's API. No Sleeper login or password.
  • Restaurant Report CardSearches go straight from your browser to NYC Open Data, the city's public inspection records.
  • Email is optionalBeyond that, the only network call is the optional email signup, which sends just your address to our email provider, Kit.
