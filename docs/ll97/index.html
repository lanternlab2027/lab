Sources (checked Nov 13, 2026)

  • Benchmarking data: NYC Open Data, LL84 Energy and Water Data Disclosure 2023 to present (latest: calendar year 2024).
  • Energy grades: DOB 2025 LL33 Data Disclosure for CY2024; grade thresholds from DOB Energy Grading (LL33).
  • 2024-2029 emissions coefficients, $268 per tCO2e and annual May 1 report: Local Law 97 of 2019 (Admin. Code §28-320.3.1.1, §28-320.3.7, §28-320.6).
  • Emissions limits by property type (2024-2029 and 2030-2034), 2030-2034 coefficients, late-filing penalty and extensions: 1 RCNY §103-14.
  • Substitute property types: DOB Article 320 Info Guide (v1.4, June 30, 2025).
  • Deadlines: DOB service notice, Deadlines for Sustainability Laws in 2026; Benchmarking (LL84); Energy Audits (LL87); LL97 overview.

This page is an informational estimate, not legal, engineering, tax or financial advice, and not a DOB determination. It uses public data that owners self-report and that can be incomplete, estimated or out of date. It does not know your building's LL97 compliance pathway (for example Article 321 for certain affordable housing and houses of worship, or later start dates), exact floor areas by use, deductions (such as offsets, which are capped at 10% of the limit, solar or beneficial electrification), approved adjustments or penalty mitigation. Before you make decisions or spend money, have a registered design professional review your building and confirm requirements and dates on nyc.gov.
