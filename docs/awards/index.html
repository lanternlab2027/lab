The awards

Every week

  • Highest scorer and the participation trophy (lowest score)
  • Biggest blowout: largest margin of victory
  • Heartbreak: narrowest loss
  • Bench warmer: most points left on the bench
  • Luckiest win: lowest score that still won
  • Unluckiest loss: highest score that still lost

Season so far

  • Power rankings: 50% all-play win %, 30% points per game, 20% last three weeks
  • Record vs all-play: your record if you played every team every week
  • Luckiest and unluckiest manager: biggest gap between the two
  • Trade & waiver king: most completed transactions
  • Season bench boss and week of the year

Season awards use completed regular-season weeks only.

Questions

Do I need to log in?

No. Sleeper league data is public to anyone with the league ID, and this page reads it through Sleeper's public, read-only API. There's no account and no password.

What happens to my data?

Your browser asks Sleeper for the league directly. Nothing goes through our servers (we don't have any), and there are no analytics, cookies or trackers. The only thing ever sent to us is your email and league ID, and only if you fill in the optional email box.

How is "points left on the bench" counted?

It's the total points scored by players on your roster who weren't in your starting lineup that week. It doesn't check whether they could legally have started, so treat it as bragging rights, not a lineup audit.

Does it work for ESPN or Yahoo?

Not yet. ESPN private leagues and Yahoo need a login, so this only covers Sleeper leagues for now.

Can I bookmark my league?

Yes. After the awards load, the page address includes your league ID. Bookmark it or drop it in the group chat and it opens straight to your league.
