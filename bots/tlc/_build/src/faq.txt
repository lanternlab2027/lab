Get a Driver's License Frequently Asked Questions 
 Expand All Collapse All 
How can I submit a new TLC Driver License application? 
Apply online at LARS . 
What do I need to complete to get a new TLC Driver License? 
Visit Get a Drivers License . 
What are the New Fitness Rules? How will these rules effect my application? 
The TLC made some changes to its license application process to make it easier and more efficient for applicants and the TLC. The new Rules include a one (1) year and three (3) year application ban depending on an applicant's TLC and DMV history. Read the New Fitness Rules (PDF) for additional information. 
How many TLC Driver license types are there? 
There are three (3) TLC driver licenses: 
TLC Driver license (allows you to drive a Yellow Cab, Green Cab, and For-Hire Vehicle) 
TLC Commuter Van Driver license 
TLC Paratransit Driver license 
Additional information for each license type is available in Get a Drivers License . 
Will I need to attend School if I am applying for a TLC Driver License (Yellow Cab, Green Cab, and For Hire Vehicle)? 
Yes. You must attend school after filing an application with the TLC. Visit Driver Education for course information. 
How can I check the status of my TLC Driver License Application? 
You can check the status of your application in TLC UP . 
How long will it take to receive my New TLC Driver License in the mail? 
The applicant will either receive his/her license in the mail, or be contacted through text, email, a letter, or TLC UP with a request for additional information which is necessary to continue the application process. 
Each application and its processing time will depend on the review of all provided documents, a review of the individual's fingerprint response report, any prior TLC license revocations, and/or outstanding requirements or clearances from the Department of Motor Vehicles, Parking Violations Bureau, and TLC. A fitness hearing may be required before a decision on your license application is made. 
Applications are valid for ninety calendar days from the date it is submitted to TLC. If an applicant does not complete all the licensing requirements within the ninety calendar day period, the application will be denied. All application fees are non-refundable. 
My TLC license was mailed but I have not received it. What do I do? 
You will need to wait ten (10) business days from the date your license was mailed. Log into   TLC UP   to see the date that the license was mailed. If after 10 business days you still have not received your new license, you will need to   schedule an appointment   to visit our LIC facility located at: 
NYC TLC
31-00 47th Avenue, 3rd floor
Long Island City NY 11101 
to submit a completed and signed   Credentials Form   (PDF). Read the instructions on page 2. Please note that all required documents must be brought to your appointment. 
A   temporary license   will be issued to all Drivers who carry a   NYS DMV License . The replacement license will be mailed at a later date as your photo will be derived from your NYS DMV Drivers license. 
A   replacement license   will be issued to all Drivers who carry a   Non-NYS DMV License . This replacement license will be issued at the LIC facility on the day of your appointment.
