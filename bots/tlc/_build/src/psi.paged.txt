WHAT DO I NEED TO KNOW ABOUT THE TLC DRIVER LICENSE EXAM?
• The TLC Driver License Exam is an electronic exam using a computer, mouse, and keyboard. Click
here for a video of the tutorial.
• The exam is 80 questions and you will have 2 hours to complete it.
• The TLC Driver License exam is $49 for each exam, which you will pay to PSI.
• EXAM FEES ARE NOT REFUNDABLE OR TRANSFERABLE.
• You will lose the $49 you paid for the TLC Driver License Exam if you:
1. Do not cancel your appointment at least 2 days before the scheduled exam appointment.
2. Do not appear for your exam appointment.
3. Arrive after exam appointment start time; or
4. Do not present proper identification when you arrive for the exam.

=====PAGE 6=====
• You may take the TLC Driver License Exam as many times as you like until your TLC application
expires. You can find the date your TLC application expires at www.nyc.gov/tlcup.
• You must arrive at the PSI test center 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to take the
TLC Driver License Exam.
HOW DO I RESCHEDULE OR CANCEL MY APPOINTMENT FOR THE TLC DRIVER LICENSE EXAM?
You may cancel or reschedule your exam appointment up until 2 days before the appointment at no extra
cost. If you cancel up to 2 days before the appointment you will receive a full refund within 4 business days.
Go to https://test-takers.psiexams.com/nyctlc to log into your account to reschedule or cancel.
IF I FAIL HOW DO I SCHEDULE AGAIN?
If you fail, you may test as many times as needed until your TLC Driver License Application Expires. You
can find the date your TLC application expires at www.nyc.gov/tlcup.
You must wait 24 hours if you want to take the TLC Driver License Exam again. Log into your PSI account
at https://www.psiexams.com/NYCTLC to schedule a new appointment to take your TLC Driver License
Exam. You will need to pay the $49 exam fee for every attempt.
WHAT IF I ARRIVE LATE OR MISS MY APPOINTMENT TO TAKE THE TLC DRIVER LICENSE EXAM?
You will lose the $49 you paid for the TLC Driver License Exam.
WHAT DO I NEED TO KNOW ABOUT THE DAY I TAKE THE TLC DRIVER LICENSE EXAM?
You must arrive at least 15 minutes before your exam appointment time to go through the check-in process. If
you arrive after your appointment time, you will not be able to take the TLC Driver License Exam and you will
lose the $49 you paid for the exam.
PSI will check your State-issued Driver’s License to confirm your identity as well as take your picture and record
your signature.
REQUIRED IDENTIFICATION
The State-issued Driver’s License must match the first and last name under which the applicant is
registered. The ID must contain the applicant’s photo and not be expired.
SIGN IN REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS
You will be required to complete a TLC Sign-in Registration Form to be let into the exam room.
SECURITY PROCEDURES
The following security procedures will apply during the exam:
▪ You are not allowed to take a break while testing.
▪ Applicants may take only approved items into the exam room.
▪ All personal belongings of applicants should be placed in the secure storage provided at each site prior
to entering the exam room. Personal belongings include, but are not limited to, the following items:
- Electronic devices of any type, including cellular / mobile phones, recording devices,
electronic watches, cameras, pagers, laptop computers, tablet computers (e.g., iPads),
music players (e.g., iPods), smart watches, radios, or electronic games.

=====PAGE 7=====
- Bulky or loose clothing or coats that could be used to conceal recording devices or notes. For
security purposes outerwear such as, but not limited to: open sweaters, cardigans, shawls, scarves,
hoodies, vests, jackets and coats are not permitted in the exam room. In the event you are asked
to remove the outerwear, appropriate attire, such as a shirt or blouse should be worn
underneath.
- Hats or headgear not worn for religious reasons or as religious apparel, including hats, baseball
caps, or visors.
- Other personal items, including purses, notebooks, reference or reading material, briefcases,
backpacks, wallets, pens, pencils, other writing devices, food, drinks, and good luck items.
▪ Person(s) accompanying an exam applicant may not wait in the exam center, inside the building or on
the building's property. This applies to any type of guest, including applicants’ children, friends, family,
colleagues, or instructors.
▪ Smoking, eating, or drinking is not allowed in the exam center.
▪ During the check in process, all applicants will be asked if they have any prohibited items with them.
Applicants may also be asked to empty their pockets and turn them out for the proctor to show the
proctor they are empty. The proctor may also ask applicants to lift up the ends of their sleeves and the
bottoms of their pant legs to ensure that notes or recording devices are not being hidden there.
▪ Proctors will also carefully inspect eyeglass frames, tie tacks, or any other apparel that could be used
to harbor a recording device. Proctors will ask to inspect any such items in applicants’ pockets.
▪ If prohibited items are found during check-in, applicants shall put them in the provided secure storage
or return these items to their vehicle. PSI will not be responsible for the security of any personal
belongings or prohibited items.
▪ Any applicant possessing prohibited items in the exam room shall immediately have his or her test
results invalidated, and PSI will notify the TLC.
▪ Any applicant seen giving or receiving assistance on an exam, found with unauthorized materials, or
who violates any security regulations will be asked to surrender all exam materials and to leave the
exam center. All such instances will be reported to the exam sponsor.
▪ Copying or communicating exam content is violation of an applicant’s contract with PSI, and federal
and state law. Either may result in the disqualification of exam results and may lead to legal action.
▪ Once applicants have been seated and the exam begins, they may leave the exam room only to use
the restroom, and only after obtaining permission from the proctor. Applicant will not receive extra time
to complete the exam.
IN THE EXAM ROOM
PSI test center staff will walk you into the exam room to a computer where you will take the TLC Driver
License Exam. All directions will be given directly on the computer.
PSI will provide applicants with the following materials:
1. Geographia NYC Five Borough Street Atlas
2. Manhattan Address Key
3. A blank piece of paper
4. Pencil or pen
These items will be collected by PSI after you finish the exam. Calculators are not allowed.
WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I TAKE THE TLC DRIVER LICENSE EXAM?
You will find out if you passed the TLC Driver License Exam on the computer screen after you submit your
exam.
PSI will email you your exam results 48 hours after you completed the exam. If you did not pass the TLC
Driver License Exam, PSI will send you a summary report with topic areas you need to study more, such as
geography.

EXAM ACCOMMODATIONS
All exam centers are able to provide access in accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) of
1990.
Requirements for exam accommodation requests:
You are required to submit documentation from the medical authority or learning institution that rendered a
diagnosis. Verification must be uploaded to PSI on the letterhead stationery of the authority or specialist
and include the following:
 Description of the disability and limitations related to testing
 Recommended accommodation/modification
 Name, title, and telephone number of the medical authority or specialist
 Original signature of the medical authority or specialist
Applicants with disabilities or those who may have difficulty taking the exam should request alternative
arrangements by Clicking Here.
If you are requesting an exam accommodation, do not schedule the exam until PSI has confirmed your
request via email.
EXAM TEST CENTER CLOSING FOR AN EMERGENCY
If severe weather or another emergency forces an exam center to close on a scheduled exam date, your
exam will be rescheduled. PSI personnel will attempt to contact you in this situation. However, you may
check the status of your exam appointment by calling (833) 892-5440 or viewing the website at https://test-
takers.psiexams.com/nyctlc. Every effort will be made to reschedule your exam at a convenient time as soon
as possible. You will not be penalized. Your exam will be rescheduled at no additional charge to you.
