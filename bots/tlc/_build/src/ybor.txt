Yellow Taxi Passenger Bill of Rights 
As a taxi rider, you have the right to: 
Not be refused service based on your race, ethnicity, cultural background, disability, gender or sexual orientation; 
Travel with a service animal; 
Be driven to any destination in NYC, Westchester, Nassau or Newark Airport; 
A driver licensed by the TLC and whose license is displayed inside the vehicle; 
A clean and safe taxicab with working seatbelts for all passengers; 
Take the most direct route to your destination or follow the route of your choice; 
Request the driver adjust the heat, air-conditioning and radio; 
Use the E-ZPass lanes and pay the E-ZPass toll rate; 
Pay by cash, credit or debit card; 
Decide whether to tip and how much to tip your driver; 
Not pay an additional amount for baggage or help loading your belongings; 
A driver who is safe and courteous and obeys all traffic laws; 
A driver who does not use a cell phone (hand-held or hands-free) while driving; 
A driver who knows city geography; 
Clean air, free of smoke and scent; 
A quiet trip without horn honking.
