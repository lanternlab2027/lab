TLC Driver License Requirements:
You must complete all these steps within 90 days
from the date of your application, or your
application will be denied:
1. At least 19 years old
2. Proof of a Valid DMV Chauffer’s License–
for NY State, this is Class A, B, C, or E.
Check with other states.
3. You must have a valid Social Security
Number
4. No fines or fees owed to NYS DMV or TLC
5. Proof of completion of Defensive Driving
Course (DDC) within 3 years before
applying.
6. Complete Wheelchair Accessible Training
7. Complete a 24-hour course and pass the test
8. Have no more than 5 points on your DMV
license within a 15-month period going back
from your application date
9. TLC Medical Form completed by licensed
physician that is no older than 90 days
10. Take and pass a drug test
11. View the Sex Trafficking Awareness Training
video on the TLC website
12. Must be fingerprinted. Fingerprints
will be reviewed for criminal history
13. Have no pending criminal charges that will
not be resolved within 90 days of the
application
14. If DMV Driver’s License is not from NY
State, out-of-state DMV driving abstract
that is no older than 90 days
15. If NY State DMV Driver’s License is less
than 1 year old, most recent out-of-state
DMV driving abstract
***Do Not Apply If***
In the last year you were:
1. In 4 or more traffic crashes.
2. Convicted of unlicensed operation of a motor
vehicle.
3. Found not fit to hold a TLC license (one year
from date of application).
4. Failed a drug test (one year from date of
failed drug test)
In the last 3 years your TLC license was:
1. Revoked (3 years from the date it was
revoked)
2. Surrendered or expired while revocation
charges were pending (3 years from date of
surrender or expiration)
YOUR NAME MUST BE IDENTICAL ON
ALL DOCUMENTS: Your First, Middle,
and Last names must be exactly the same
on all documents.
