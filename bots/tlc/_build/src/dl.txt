Are You Qualified? 
Minimum age: 19 
Have a Valid DMV Chauffer's license- Commercial Classes (A, B, C) or For-hire Class (E) 
You must have a valid Social Security Number 
No fines or fees owed to NYS DMV or TLC 
No more than 5 points on your DMV license within a 15 month period back from your most recent conviction date 
To see the "fit to hold a license" requirements look at Fitness Rules Notice . 
Out-of-State Applicants / Licensees 
 NEW MANDATORY REQUIREMENT Out-of-State Driver License Applicants/Licensees (New, Renewals, and Replacements) 
Effective Date: October 1, 2025 
Starting on October 1, 2025, all out-of-state Applicants/Licensees—whether applying for a new, renewal, or replacement TLC Driver's License—must complete a new step to allow the TLC to obtain the necessary Client ID number for their application. 
The Applicant/Licensee must follow these steps to meet this new requirement: 
 Step 1: Get the Required Form  
The Applicant/Licensee must download the ID-5 form, which is available here ID-5 Form , and bring it with them to the DMV office. The applicant/Licensee does not fill out the form . 
 Step 2: Visit a New York State DMV Office  
Take the ID-5 form to any New York State DMV office. No appointment is necessary. 
The Applicant/Licensee must present their original identity documents (proofs of name, date of birth, and signature) to the staff for verification. 
The DMV staff will complete the ID-5 form and take the Applicant's photo. 
There is no charge for having your picture taken at the DMV. 
This action allows the DMV to generate or validate the Client ID number that TLC requires. 
 Step 3: Get the Receipt (FS-6T)  
After the DMV process is complete, the Applicant/Licensee will be given a receipt called an FS-6T. 
It is essential to keep this receipt. 
 Step 4: Upload to TLCUP   
The Applicant/Licensee must log into their TLCUP account and upload the FS-6T receipt. 
This receipt confirms that the step to obtaining the Client ID number has been completed, and it is required to proceed with your TLC Driver’s License application. 
  Step 5: (For Replacement Requests Only) Email the Receipt 
If the Licensee is requesting a license replacement, they must send the FS-6T receipt via email to licensinginquiries@tlc.nyc.gov 
Reminder: Applications submitted on or after October 1, 2025, will not be processed until the FS-6T receipt has been successfully uploaded to TLCUP.

Step by Step instructions after you’ve applied. 
Now that you have submitted your application and payment through LARS, let us help you with the next steps. 
Please remember that all fees paid for your license are non-refundable. This applies even if you do not complete the process or if it is determined that you are not eligible. In addition, please do not submit duplicate applications if you already have an application on file or if you are already licensed. Any duplicate applications will be voided, and you will lose the fees you paid. 
From the date you submitted your application, you have 90 days to complete all remaining requirements, or your application will be denied. 
 Please also keep in mind that our licensing staff will only begin reviewing your application and documentation once every single requirement has been fully completed and submitted. 
 Please follow these steps within 30 days to expedite review and approval of your application: 
Make an appointment to get your fingerprints taken by visiting https://uenroll.identogo.com/workflows/15425Y . 
Complete an approved 6-hour New York State DMV defensive driving course . 
Have your medical exam completed and signed by a licensed physician using the TLC medical certification form. 
Complete your drug screening at a TLC-approved testing facility. 
Attend the 24-hour driver education course and pass the TLC driver exam. 
Complete the Passenger Assistance and Wheelchair Accessible Vehicle (WAV) training: Take the required accessible vehicle training course. 
If your DMV driver license is from outside New York State, please refer to the instructions on our website under "Out-of-State Applicants / Licensees" to make sure you complete the additional DMV requirements and obtain your driving record abstract. 
 Completing these remaining steps within 60 days of applying will ensure sufficient time for our team to review your file and reach out if any additional documentation is needed. 
Complete the sex trafficking awareness training: Review and complete the short online awareness training. 
Clear any outstanding tickets or fines: Ensure that all parking, camera, and traffic tickets are fully resolved. 
If applicable, obtain child support clearance: Provide a clearance letter from the Office of Child Support Services (OCSS / OCE) confirming that you do not owe any past-due child support. 
Upload your required documents: Submit your course certificates, medical form, and all other required paperwork (including child support clearance letter, if applicable) through TLCUP ( www.nyc.gov/tlcup ). 
What to expect next: 
Once you have completed all requirements, our team will review your application. If no further documentation is needed, your application will be approved, and an email confirmation will be sent to the email address you provided. A physical copy of your license will be mailed via the US Postal Service and should arrive within two weeks following your approval.
