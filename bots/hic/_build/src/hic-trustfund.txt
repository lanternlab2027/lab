Home Improvement Business Trust Fund
Enrollment
Applicants who enroll in the Trust Fund for the first time must pay a $200 fee to the NYC
Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP) at the time they apply.
Note: Trust Fund participants who remain in the Fund do not have to pay again when renewing
their license if the Fund had more than $2 million as of July of the prior year.

All payments to the Trust Fund will be held in trust by the Fund for the benefit of the licensed
industry and are nonrefundable as long as the Fund exists. If the Fund dissolves, Fund monies
will be distributed equally to Fund contributors who are licensed at the time the Fund dissolves.
Under certain conditions, DCWP may require that money held in the Trust Fund be used
to pay amounts owed to consumers and fines owed to the Department. Additional information
is available under 6 RCNY § 2-224. Any payment of awards, fines, settlement agreements,
claims, and/or judgments from the Fund does not relieve the licensee from legal obligations to
satisfy DCWP orders, court judgments, and all applicable local, state, and federal rules and
laws.
