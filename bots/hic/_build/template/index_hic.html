What the real exam is like

Questions and pass mark
The exam has 30 multiple-choice questions, and you need at least 21 correct to pass. It covers four areas: the basics of the City's home improvement business law, contracts and cancellations, advertising and selling practices, and general industry and business knowledge.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 4; Examination Guide, PDF p. 1
Time limit
DCWP does not publish a time limit in its checklist, exam FAQ or Examination Guide. Ask ExamBuilder when you book. The mock exam on this site uses a practice pace of one minute per question.
Who takes it
Only new applicants take the exam; renewals do not. Only one person per business needs to pass: the sole proprietor, a general partner, a corporate officer, a principal, director or member, or a shareholder owning 10% or more of the stock. You confirm with DCWP who will take it when you apply.
Sources: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 1, 4; DCWP license checklist
Fees
The license application fee covers up to two exam attempts. If you fail twice, a $50 fee buys two more attempts. The license itself runs for 2 years and expires on February 28 of odd years, so the license fee is $50 to $250 depending on when you apply ($200 for a full 24 months). You also join the Home Improvement Business Trust Fund for $200 or file a $20,000 surety bond. Card payments carry a nonrefundable 2% convenience fee.
Source: DCWP license checklist
Booking and where
ExamBuilder runs the exam. You can book 24 hours after DCWP processes your completed application, online at dcaschedule.exambuilder.com or by phone at (646) 974-8244. You then have 60 days to take and pass it. Test centers in multiple locations citywide are open 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., seven days a week. Applicants granted a disability or language accommodation test at the DCWP Licensing Center, 42 Broadway.
Sources: DCWP license checklist; DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 1–2
Attempts and no-shows
After a first failure, you must wait 24 hours before booking again. To reschedule, ask by 5:00 p.m. the day before your exam. If you miss an exam without rescheduling, it counts as a fail.
Source: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF pp. 2–3
On the day
Bring one government-issued photo ID with your signature. ExamBuilder gives you your score and a pass or fail result as soon as you finish, and reports the result to DCWP.
Source: DCWP Exam FAQs, PDF p. 3

Heads-up on permits: the Examination Guide is dated June 2021. Since June 2026, contractors must also give owners a written permit disclosure (Local Law 181 of 2025). DCWP's newer forms say the contractor must make sure the "appropriate party" gets each permit, which can be the contractor or another licensed professional. The 2021 guide says the prime contractor secures all permits. This bank skips questions where the old and new wording conflict.

DCWP rules and fees change. Always check the latest DCWP checklist before you apply.
